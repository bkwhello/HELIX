# Purpose

This document defines the authoritative operational state owned by
Reservation Management.

It specifies:

- the states that may exist;
- the legal transitions between states;
- the conditions under which transitions may occur;
- the invariants that shall always remain true;
- the business events generated by state transitions.

This document is the single source of truth for reservation lifecycle behavior.

---

# Ownership

Reservation Management is the sole authoritative owner of:

- Reservation State
- Reservation State Transition
- Reservation Lifecycle

No other capability may redefine reservation state.

Other capabilities may observe or reference reservation state.

---

# State Principles

## SP-01

A reservation shall always exist in exactly one operational state.

---

## SP-02

A reservation shall never exist without a Reservation Identity.

---

## SP-03

State changes shall occur only through valid transitions.

---

## SP-04

Every successful state transition shall generate at least one business event.

---

## SP-05

Illegal transitions shall never partially update reservation state.

---

## SP-06

Historical state changes remain traceable through events.

---

# State Definitions

---

## Proposed

### Meaning

The reservation exists but has not yet become operationally confirmed.

### Entered By

- Create Reservation

### May Transition To

- Confirmed
- Cancelled

---

## Confirmed

### Meaning

The reservation is accepted by the restaurant.

Operational planning may proceed.

### Entered By

- Confirm Reservation

### May Transition To

- Completed
- Cancelled

---

## Cancelled

### Meaning

The reservation is no longer expected.

Operational planning shall ignore it.

### Entered By

- Cancel Reservation

### Terminal

Yes

---

## Completed

### Meaning

The guest visit has finished.

Historical information remains available.

### Entered By

- Complete Reservation

### Terminal

Yes

# Invalid Transitions

The following transitions are prohibited.

| From | To | Reason |
|------|----|---------|
| Cancelled | Proposed | Terminal state |
| Cancelled | Confirmed | Terminal state |
| Completed | Proposed | Historical integrity |
| Completed | Confirmed | Historical integrity |
| Completed | Cancelled | Visit already completed |

---

# Transition Preconditions

## Create Reservation

Requires

- Reservation Contact
- Reservation Date
- Reservation Time
- Party Size

---

## Confirm Reservation

Requires

- Existing reservation
- Reservation not cancelled
- Reservation not completed

---

## Cancel Reservation

Requires

- Existing reservation

---

## Complete Reservation

Requires

- Reservation confirmed
- Guest service finished

---

# State Invariants

## INV-01

Every reservation has exactly one Reservation Identity.

---

## INV-02

Every reservation belongs to exactly one Service Period.

---

## INV-03

A reservation exists in exactly one operational state.

---

## INV-04

Terminal states cannot transition to non-terminal states.

---

## INV-05

State transitions are atomic.

A transition either completes entirely or not at all.

---

## INV-06

Reservation Identity never changes.

---

## INV-07

Reservation history is never destroyed.

# Failure Behaviour

If a transition fails

- previous state remains unchanged;
- no partial update occurs;
- no transition event is generated;
- failure is returned explicitly.

---

# Recovery Rules

If infrastructure failure occurs after transition validation but before persistence

the reservation shall remain in its previous state.

Recovery shall never create duplicate reservations.

Recovery shall never skip intermediate states.

---

# Future States (Deferred)

The following lifecycle states are intentionally excluded from MVP.

- Pending Confirmation
- No Show
- Arrived
- Seated
- Waiting List
- Expired

These belong to future engineering increments.

# Open Questions

Engineering validation required for:

- automatic confirmation policy;

- no-show lifecycle;
- reopening cancelled reservations;
- automatic completion;
- reservation expiration.
