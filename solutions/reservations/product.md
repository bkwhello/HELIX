# Vision

Create the most operationally intelligent restaurant reservation platform by combining centralized reservation management, custom floorplan optimization, AI-assisted operations, and seamless integration with external reservation channels.

---

# Mission

Enable restaurants to manage reservations from every source through one authoritative operational system that improves guest experience, staff efficiency, and business intelligence.

# Capability Goal

Provide one operational reservation platform that becomes the authoritative source for reservation management regardless of where bookings originate.

---

# Core Problems

Restaurants today often experience:

- reservations spread across multiple systems;
- manual table planning;
- duplicate data entry;
- poor visibility of changes;
- missed guest information;
- limited operational insight;
- dependency on third-party reservation providers.

HELIX Reservations addresses these problems through centralized operational management.

---

# Product Scope

The product owns:

- reservations;
- reservation lifecycle;
- table assignments;
- floor planning;
- reservation sources;
- operational reservation workflow;
- guest visit history (reservation-related);
- seating optimization.

The product does not own:

- payment processing;
- financial accounting;
- inventory;
- payroll;
- kitchen production;
- marketing campaigns.

---

# Product Principles

1. Restaurant data belongs to the restaurant.
2. External reservation platforms are integrations.
3. One reservation has one authoritative identity.
4. The floorplan is the operational center.
5. Every feature must reduce operational effort.
6. AI assists rather than replaces staff.
7. Manual override is always possible.

---

# Success Measures

The product should:

- reduce manual work;
- reduce reservation mistakes;
- reduce overbooking;
- improve table utilization;
- improve guest experience;
- improve operational awareness;
- enable future AI capabilities.
