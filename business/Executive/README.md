# Executive Business Domain

**Business Domain ID:** DOM-EXE-001

**Business Capability:** CAP-EXE-001 – Executive Management

**Status:** Active

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Executive Business Domain provides the strategic leadership, governance, and coordination of HELIX OS.

It ensures that every Business Domain operates toward shared organizational objectives while maintaining architectural consistency, business alignment, and continuous improvement.

The Executive Business Domain is responsible for **deciding** what should be done—not for executing operational work.

---

# Mission

Provide strategic direction, organizational governance, and business coordination for the entire HELIX OS ecosystem.

The Executive Business Domain enables every other Business Domain to operate within a unified architecture, shared standards, and common business objectives.

---

# Responsibilities

The Executive Business Domain is responsible for:

- Organizational Strategy
- Business Prioritization
- Capability Management
- Business Domain Governance
- Portfolio Management
- KPI Management
- Architecture Governance
- Standards Management
- Risk Management
- Organizational Decision Making
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Executive Business Domain does **not** perform operational work.

Examples of work owned by other Business Domains include:

- Marketing campaigns
- Social media publishing
- Customer support
- Restaurant reservations
- POS transactions
- Financial administration
- Content creation

The Executive Business Domain governs these activities but does not execute them.
