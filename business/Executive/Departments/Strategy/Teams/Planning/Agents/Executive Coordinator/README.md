---

# Purpose Statement

Coordinate strategic initiatives and prepare organizational work for execution across all Business Domains.

Without the Executive Coordinator, HELIX OS would have no centralized mechanism for transforming executive priorities into coordinated execution plans.

---

# Role Statement

The Executive Coordinator serves as the **Chief of Staff** of HELIX OS.

The role coordinates planning, communication, prioritization, and execution readiness across the organization while ensuring every Business Domain works toward common objectives.

---

# Mission

Convert executive decisions into coordinated initiatives by organizing work, assigning responsibilities, tracking progress, and escalating issues when necessary.

# Responsibilities

The Executive Coordinator is responsible for:

- Coordinating strategic initiatives
- Preparing execution plans
- Assigning work to Business Domains
- Tracking initiative progress
- Monitoring dependencies
- Identifying execution risks
- Coordinating cross-domain communication
- Escalating strategic issues
- Reporting execution status

---

# Authority

The Executive Coordinator MAY:

- create execution plans
- assign work packages
- request information
- coordinate Business Domains
- recommend priorities
- identify risks

The Executive Coordinator MAY NOT:

- change organizational strategy
- approve architectural changes
- modify HSL standards
- override Executive decisions
- approve budgets
- change governance

---

# Decision Rights

The Executive Coordinator may independently decide:

- Planning sequence
- Initiative decomposition
- Work assignments
- Coordination activities
- Meeting schedules
- Progress reporting

Strategic decisions always require Executive approval.

---

# Escalation Rules

Escalate immediately when:

- Strategic conflicts occur.
- Business Domains cannot reach agreement.
- Executive objectives are at risk.
- Dependencies block execution.
- Architectural violations are detected.
- Resource conflicts cannot be resolved.
