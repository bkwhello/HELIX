# Planning Team

**Team ID:** TEAM-PLAN-001

**Department:** DEP-STR-001 – Strategy Department

**Business Domain:** DOM-EXE-001 – Executive Business Domain

**Status:** Active

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Planning Team converts organizational strategy into executable work.

It bridges the gap between strategic direction and operational execution by transforming objectives into initiatives, priorities, milestones, and work packages that Business Domains can execute.

Without the Planning Team, HELIX OS would have no structured mechanism for turning strategy into action.

---

# Mission

Prepare the organization for successful execution by producing clear, prioritized, and measurable plans.

The Planning Team ensures that every Business Domain receives actionable work aligned with organizational objectives.

---

# Responsibilities

The Planning Team is responsible for:

- Strategic planning
- Initiative planning
- Work decomposition
- Priority definition
- Dependency analysis
- Milestone planning
- Cross-domain coordination
- Execution readiness

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Planning Team does not:

- Execute operational work
- Build software
- Publish content
- Manage customers
- Execute marketing campaigns
- Perform financial operations

Its role is to prepare work, not to perform it.

---

# Team Structure

The Planning Team currently consists of:

```text
Planning Team
│
├── Executive Coordinator
├── Strategic Planner
├── Roadmap Planner
└── Initiative Prioritizer
```

Each Agent performs a specialized function within the Strategic Planning Process.

---

# Primary Process

The Planning Team owns the Strategic Planning Process.

```text
Executive Objective
        ↓
Strategic Initiative
        ↓
Work Breakdown
        ↓
Priority Assessment
        ↓
Dependency Analysis
        ↓
Execution Plan
        ↓
Business Domain Assignment
```
