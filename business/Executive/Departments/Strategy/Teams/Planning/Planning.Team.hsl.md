# Purpose Statement

Without the Planning Team, HELIX OS would have no structured process for converting strategic objectives into executable initiatives.

---

# Purpose

The Planning Team transforms strategic direction into actionable work packages for Business Domains.

It bridges the gap between executive strategy and operational execution.

---

# Mission

Translate organizational objectives into prioritized initiatives, execution plans, and work assignments that can be delegated to Business Domains and their Teams.

---

# Scope

The Planning Team owns the Strategic Planning Process.

This includes:

- Initiative planning
- Priority definition
- Work decomposition
- Dependency identification
- Resource estimation
- Milestone planning
- Cross-domain planning
- Execution readiness

---

# Responsibilities

## Strategic Planning

Convert executive objectives into executable initiatives.

---

## Initiative Definition

Define business initiatives.

Break initiatives into manageable work packages.

---

## Prioritization

Determine execution order.

Identify business impact.

Recommend priorities.

---

## Dependency Management

Identify dependencies between Business Domains.

Identify execution constraints.

Coordinate sequencing.

---

## Resource Planning

Estimate effort.

Recommend resource allocation.

Identify capability gaps.

---

## Execution Preparation

Ensure initiatives are sufficiently defined before operational execution begins.

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Planning Team shall not:

- Execute initiatives
- Publish content
- Build workflows
- Develop software
- Operate Business Domains

Execution belongs to downstream Teams.

The Planning Team answers one question:

**"How do we transform strategy into executable work?"**

It prepares execution.
