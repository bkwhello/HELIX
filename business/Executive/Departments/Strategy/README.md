# Strategy Department

**Department ID:** DEP-STR-001

**Business Domain:** DOM-EXE-001 – Executive Business Domain

**Status:** Active

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Strategy Department transforms organizational vision into actionable direction.

It bridges executive intent with operational planning by defining objectives, priorities, initiatives, and long-term roadmaps.

Without the Strategy Department, HELIX OS would have no structured process for converting strategic goals into executable work.

---

# Mission

Translate the organization's vision into measurable objectives, strategic initiatives, and prioritized plans that guide every Business Domain.

---

# Responsibilities

The Strategy Department is responsible for:

- Strategic Planning
- Organizational Objectives
- Long-Term Roadmaps
- Business Prioritization
- Initiative Definition
- Cross-Domain Alignment
- Strategic Reviews
- Goal Management

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Strategy Department does not:

- Execute operational work
- Manage daily business activities
- Publish content
- Operate workflows
- Manage technology implementations
- Perform financial administration

Execution belongs to Teams and Agents within the appropriate Business Domains.
