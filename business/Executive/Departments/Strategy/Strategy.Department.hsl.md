# Purpose Statement

Without the Strategy Department, HELIX OS would have no structured mechanism for transforming organizational vision into strategic objectives, priorities, and executable plans.

---

# Purpose

The Strategy Department converts executive intent into measurable organizational direction.

It ensures that every Business Domain works toward shared objectives through structured planning, prioritization, and long-term roadmap development.

---

# Mission

Provide strategic planning and organizational direction by translating executive decisions into executable initiatives and measurable objectives.

---

# Scope

The Strategy Department governs:

- Strategic Planning
- Organizational Objectives
- Long-Term Roadmaps
- Initiative Definition
- Strategic Reviews
- Business Prioritization
- Cross-Domain Alignment

The Strategy Department does not execute operational business activities.

---

# Responsibilities

## Strategic Planning

- Develop strategic plans
- Define organizational priorities
- Align business initiatives

---

## Objective Management

- Define organizational objectives
- Maintain objective hierarchy
- Measure strategic progress

---

## Roadmap Management

- Create long-term roadmaps
- Sequence initiatives
- Maintain strategic timelines

---

## Initiative Planning

- Define strategic initiatives
- Prepare execution proposals
- Coordinate cross-domain planning

---

## Cross-Domain Alignment

- Align Business Domains
- Resolve planning conflicts
- Coordinate organizational priorities

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Strategy Department shall not:

- Execute operational work
- Manage daily workflows
- Publish content
- Operate marketing campaigns
- Manage technology implementations
- Perform customer support
- Execute financial operations

Execution belongs to operational Business Domains.
