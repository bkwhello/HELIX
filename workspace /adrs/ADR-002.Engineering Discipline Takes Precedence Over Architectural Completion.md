# ADR-002 – Engineering Discipline Takes Precedence Over Architectural Completion

Status

Accepted

Date

<DATE>

---

# Context

During the development of the HELIX Organizational Ontology, opportunities arose to introduce architectural concepts into the Reference Model before they had completed the Engineering Investigation lifecycle.

Doing so would produce a complete-looking architecture, but at the cost of engineering rigor and traceability.

HELIX prioritizes validated knowledge over architectural completeness.

---

# Decision

Architectural artifacts shall only contain knowledge that has successfully completed the HELIX Engineering Discipline.

Reference artifacts shall never be completed by assumption, intuition, convention, or design preference.

When evidence is insufficient, the investigation shall remain open or be suspended until additional evidence becomes available.

---

# Rationale

Engineering integrity requires that architectural knowledge is earned rather than designed.

A partially complete but fully validated architecture is preferable to a complete architecture containing unvalidated concepts.

This preserves:

- traceability,
- reproducibility,
- falsifiability,
- engineering discipline.

---

# Consequences

Positive

- Every architectural concept is traceable to evidence.
- Reference artifacts remain trustworthy.
- Engineering investigations may legitimately remain incomplete.

Negative

- Architectural completeness may progress more slowly.
- Temporary gaps in the Reference Model are expected.
