ADR-001

Title

Reference Artifacts Shall Not Introduce New Knowledge

Decision

Reference artifacts (AO, RM, Specifications and Standards) may only contain concepts and relationships that have been independently validated through Engineering Investigations.

Motivation

During M-002 consolidation we observed that architectural documents were beginning to include inferred concepts (Governance, Outcome, Learning) before dedicated investigations had been completed.

Consequence

Reference documents become summaries of validated knowledge rather than sources of new architectural knowledge.
