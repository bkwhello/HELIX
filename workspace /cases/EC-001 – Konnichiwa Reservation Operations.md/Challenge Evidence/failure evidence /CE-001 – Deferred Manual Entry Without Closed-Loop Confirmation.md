### CE-001 – Deferred Manual Entry Without Closed-Loop Confirmation

Source

O-004 – Deferred Manual Reservation Entry

Additional Source

O-005 – Reservation Information Remained in Group App Without Guestplan Entry

Status

Open

Initial Classification

Uncertainty

Current Classification

Reported Failure Evidence

----

#### Description

A future reservation request made directly on the restaurant floor may pass from the Supervisor to the Manager through handwritten information, a photograph, and a group messaging application before being entered into Guestplan.

Entry may be deferred because the Manager may receive the request during private time.

The Supervisor does not normally receive confirmation that Guestplan entry has been completed.

O-005 established a reported actual occurrence in which reservation information remained in the group app and was not entered into Guestplan.


#### Evidence History

Initial State

The possibility that the Manager might forget to enter a reservation had been reported, but no actual occurrence had yet been established within EC-001.

Classification

Uncertainty

Current State

An actual occurrence has now been reported by the Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

The reservation information remained available in the group app but was not entered into Guestplan.

The missing reservation was discovered only when the guest physically arrived at the restaurant expecting the reservation.

Classification

Reported Failure Evidence


#### Current Evidence State

Established through direct organizational account:

- The manual transfer pathway exists.
- Entry may be deferred.
- The Supervisor does not normally receive confirmation of completed Guestplan entry.
- An actual occurrence has been reported where reservation information remained in the group app without being entered into Guestplan.
- The missing reservation was discovered only when the guest physically arrived at the restaurant expecting the reservation.

Not yet established:

- documentary corroboration through inspection of the original group-app message,
- whether the guest was ultimately seated,
- whether the occurrence created a capacity conflict,
- whether the floor reservation plan was affected,
- frequency of occurrence,
- whether this has occurred through other reservation channels.
