# Explicit Boundaries
---

## Organizational Scope

The case investigates the organizational handling of restaurant reservations at Konnichiwa.

The initial scope includes:

- reservation intake,
- reservation confirmation,
- reservation modification,
- reservation cancellation,
- guest information relevant to reservation handling,
- table availability and allocation,
- walk-in handling where it interacts with reservation operations,
- communication between reservation handling and restaurant operations,
- operational preparation based on reservation information,
- current actors, systems, tools, Work Objects, relationships, governance, and outcomes involved in reservation handling.

## Temporal Scope

The case investigates the current operational reality of reservation handling at Konnichiwa as observed during the case period beginning 11 July 2026.

Historical information may be used as evidence where relevant but shall be explicitly distinguished from current observations.

## Engineering Scope

The initial lifecycle scope is:

Observation
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Justified Organizational Claims
        ↓
Organizational Understanding
        ↓
Organizational Diagnosis

Organizational Design and Organizational Transformation are excluded from the initial lifecycle scope.

The case must first establish a justified Organizational Diagnosis before any reservation-system design may begin.

## Out of Scope

Initially out of scope:

- software architecture for a new reservation system,
- software implementation,
- POS system design,
- technical API integration,
- vendor selection,
- UI/UX design,
- database architecture,
- implementation planning,
- Organizational Design,
- Organizational Transformation.

Current interactions between reservation operations and existing operational systems remain in scope as organizational reality to be observed.
