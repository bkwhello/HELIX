# Purpose

EC-001 investigates how Konnichiwa coordinates reservations across multiple operational channels while preserving accurate operational representation inside Guestplan.

The Engineering Case applies the HELIX Organizational Engineering Process to real organizational operations under observed conditions.

The purpose of the case is not to prove HELIX correct.

The purpose is to discover, through observation, evidence, engineering reasoning and transformation, how reservation coordination actually behaves and whether organizational improvements can be justified.

Supporting, contradictory, uncertain and failure evidence are preserved equally.

---

# Engineering Question

How can Konnichiwa preserve accurate operational reservation representation while guest commitments and reservation changes arrive through multiple organizational channels?

# Current Engineering Understanding

The Engineering Case began as an investigation into a forgotten reservation.

Through engineering observation the case has expanded into the investigation of reservation coordination across multiple operational channels.

Current evidence indicates that Guestplan functions as the central operational reservation representation used during reservation preparation and floor planning.

Several reservation pathways require manual organizational completion before Guestplan reflects operational reality.

The engineering significance of those pathways differs and remains under investigation.

---

# Current Organizational Diagnosis

# Candidate Broader Condition

CBC-001 – External Reservation Reality Requiring Manual Guestplan Completion

Current observations indicate that several reservation pathways may require manual organizational completion before Guestplan accurately represents operational reservation reality.

Examples currently observed include:

- future reservation requests accepted on the restaurant floor,
- telephone reservation modifications,
- TheFork reservation transfer.

CBC-001 is preserved as a Candidate Broader Condition.

It is not yet:

- an Organizational Claim,
- Organizational Understanding,
- Organizational Diagnosis,
- or a separate Engineering Case.
