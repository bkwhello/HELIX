# Purpose
---

This Engineering Case applies the HELIX Organizational Engineering Process to the reservation operations of Konnichiwa under explicitly observed conditions.

The case exists to investigate organizational reality through disciplined observation, evidence collection, justified Organizational Claims, Organizational Understanding, and Organizational Diagnosis.

The case shall produce inspectable, traceable, and condition-aware engineering evidence capable of supporting or challenging confidence in the HELIX Engineering Discipline.

This case is not intended to prove HELIX correct.

Evidence that supports, contradicts, exceeds, or remains unexplained by HELIX shall be preserved.

The case is intended to allow organizational reality to challenge HELIX.
