#### TM-001 – WhatsApp-Based Closed-Loop Reservation Protocol

Status

Survives Conditionally as a Pilot Transformation Mechanism

Source Design

- DES-001 – Closed-Loop Future-Reservation Completion

Source Readiness Evaluation

- TR-001 – Transformation Readiness Evaluation of DES-001

Transformation Readiness Gaps Addressed

---

##### Candidate Transformation Mechanism

Use a WhatsApp group as a lightweight physical and operational representation of DES-001.

Every future reservation request accepted directly on the restaurant floor and not immediately completed in Guestplan shall be posted through a structured reservation message.

The message shall preserve:

- sufficient reservation identity,
- accepted guest commitment,
- current Guestplan completion state,
- current completion accountability,
- temporal relevance,
- and exception information where required.

The mechanism shall preserve explicit relationships between:

- submission,
- responsibility acceptance,
- Guestplan completion,
- correction,
- cancellation,
- and attention-required states.

---

##### Candidate Operational States

The mechanism shall preserve the following semantic distinctions:

- Submitted
- Accepted for Completion
- Completed
- Attention Required
- Cancelled

The final physical wording or labels remain subject to pilot evaluation.

A small state set with explicit reason information is preferred over unnecessary state complexity.

---

##### Guest Commitment and Internal State

The protocol shall distinguish:

Guest Commitment

Accepted by Konnichiwa when the future reservation request is accepted on the restaurant floor.

Guestplan Completion State

Pending until the intended reservation has been entered into Guestplan and verified.

A pending Guestplan state shall not be interpreted as absence of organizational commitment to the guest.

---

##### Structured Request Requirement

Every submitted request shall contain sufficient information to distinguish and later complete the reservation.

Candidate information may include:

- guest name,
- phone number,
- reservation date,
- reservation time,
- number of guests,
- dining area,
- allergy or operational information where relevant,
- submitting actor.

The exact minimum mandatory information set remains subject to pilot establishment.

---

##### Initial Accountability Rule

The actor submitting the future reservation request remains accountable for the request until another authorized actor or role explicitly accepts completion accountability.

Sending the information does not by itself transfer accountability.

---

##### Accountability-Transfer Rule

Accountability transfers only when an authorized actor or role explicitly accepts responsibility for completing the Guestplan entry.

The acceptance shall be inspectably linked to the original reservation request.

Vague acknowledgement, message visibility, or assumed receipt shall not establish accountability transfer.

---

##### Completion Rule

Completed shall mean:

1. the intended reservation has been entered into Guestplan;
2. the completing actor has visually verified that the reservation exists in Guestplan;
3. the essential accepted details correspond sufficiently to the intended reservation;
4. completion has been explicitly recorded in a response linked to the original request.

An attempted entry does not itself establish completion.

---

##### Attention Rule

Every unresolved request shall be reviewed before the latest safe operational point at which preparation depends on its Guestplan representation.

A candidate operational checkpoint is the rolling reservation-planning review in which:

- today is checked as priority,
- tomorrow is reviewed proactively,
- and later dates may be reviewed within the three-day preparation horizon.

Any unresolved request entering the relevant planning horizon shall become Attention Required.

The exact checkpoint remains subject to pilot evaluation.

---

##### Exception Behavior

Where completion cannot occur, the request shall remain inspectably unresolved and include the reason where relevant.

Possible reasons include:

- Guestplan unavailable,
- insufficient information,
- requested capacity unavailable,
- organizational decision required,
- responsibility not yet accepted,
- or another recorded exception.

Technical failure and organizational decision requirements shall remain distinguishable.

---

##### Historical Integrity

Essential reservation meaning shall not be silently rewritten after submission.

Corrections, changes and cancellations shall be recorded through linked messages or another inspectable mechanism that preserves historical meaning.

---

##### Message-Linking Requirement

Responsibility acceptance, completion, correction, cancellation and exception updates shall remain linked to the original reservation-request message.

Unlinked generic messages such as “done” or “completed” are insufficient.

---

##### Operational Visibility Limitation

WhatsApp is not inherently a task queue.

Reservation requests may become buried within unrelated group conversation.

TM-001 therefore requires an explicit unresolved-request review mechanism.

The existing WhatsApp group may be used initially only if message visibility remains operationally sufficient.

A dedicated reservation group or stronger shared-state mechanism may be required if unrelated message volume prevents reliable unresolved-state inspection.

---

##### Falsification Results

The following weaker interpretation was rejected:

Continue the current WhatsApp transfer and ask the Manager to confirm after Guestplan entry.

This interpretation does not ensure:

- explicit unresolved state,
- accountability continuity,
- responsibility acceptance,
- pre-service attention,
- verified completion,
- or visibility of buried requests.

The governed protocol survives conditionally.

---

##### Pilot Conditions

TM-001 shall initially be treated as a bounded pilot rather than a permanent transformation.

The pilot shall evaluate:

- whether every accepted floor request is submitted,
- whether accountability acceptance is explicit,
- whether unresolved requests remain visible,
- whether completion corresponds to actual Guestplan entry,
- whether the attention checkpoint detects unresolved requests,
- whether staff can execute the protocol during service,
- whether message volume creates visibility failure,
- and whether the mechanism introduces excessive operational burden.

---

##### TM-001 Conclusion

The existing WhatsApp environment is capable of representing DES-001 only through a governed closed-loop protocol.

Informal message transfer is insufficient and can reproduce CE-001.

TM-001 survives as a proportionate pilot transformation mechanism subject to:

- structured request representation,
- explicit state semantics,
- accountability continuity,
- explicit responsibility acceptance,
- linked completion communication,
- verified Guestplan completion,
- unresolved-request review,
- latest-safe-point attention,
- exception handling,
- and pilot evaluation.

TM-001 is not yet established as the permanent transformation mechanism.

A pilot protocol must be operationalized and tested before Organizational Transformation can be declared complete.

##### Pilot Purpose

The purpose of TP-001 is to test whether the existing WhatsApp environment can operationally realize DES-001 through a governed closed-loop protocol.

The pilot shall test whether every future reservation request accepted directly on the restaurant floor can be:

- reliably preserved,
- sufficiently identified,
- assigned explicit accountability,
- maintained in an inspectable unresolved state,
- entered into Guestplan,
- verified as completed,
- and surfaced for attention before operational preparation depends upon it.

The pilot is not intended to prove that WhatsApp is the permanent transformation mechanism.

Evidence supporting, contradicting, limiting, or exceeding the pilot shall be preserved.

---

##### Pilot Scope

TP-001 applies only to:

- future reservation requests accepted directly on the restaurant floor,
- where the reservation is not immediately entered and verified in Guestplan by an authorized actor.

TP-001 does not apply to:

- website reservations,
- Google reservations,
- TheFork reservations,
- telephone reservations unless separately authorized,
- same-day walk-ins,
- reservation modification processes outside the pilot,
- reservation cancellation processes outside the pilot,
- redesign of Guestplan,
- redesign of the physical Floor Reservation Plan.

---

##### Pilot Actors

###### PA-001 – Submitting Actor

The Submitting Actor is the organizational actor who accepts the future reservation request directly from the guest.

This will normally be:

- Supervisor,
- Manager,
- Assistant Manager,
- Owner,
- or another explicitly authorized floor-level actor.

Responsibilities:

- obtain the minimum required reservation information,
- post the structured reservation request,
- verify that the message appears in the designated WhatsApp group,
- retain accountability until another authorized actor explicitly accepts completion accountability,
- respond when information is missing or clarification is required,
- verify that unresolved requests receive attention at the required checkpoint.

---

###### PA-002 – Completion Actor

The Completion Actor is an actor authorized and competent to create the reservation in Guestplan.

Current eligible roles:

- Owner,
- Manager,
- Assistant Manager.

Responsibilities:

- explicitly accept completion accountability,
- review the submitted reservation information,
- identify missing or conflicting information,
- enter the reservation into Guestplan,
- visually verify the Guestplan entry,
- record completion through a linked WhatsApp reply,
- record an exception where completion cannot occur.

No actor becomes the Completion Actor merely by reading the message.

---

###### PA-003 – Review Actor

The Review Actor performs the unresolved-request review at the operational checkpoint.

The Review Actor may be:

- Owner,
- Manager,
- Assistant Manager,
- or another role explicitly assigned for the pilot.

Responsibilities:

- inspect unresolved pilot requests,
- compare them with Guestplan,
- identify requests lacking accepted accountability,
- identify requests lacking verified completion,
- move unresolved requests into Attention Required where necessary,
- ensure that action occurs before the latest safe operational point.

The Review Actor may also be the Completion Actor, but the responsibilities remain semantically distinct.

###### Mixed Evidence

Evidence containing both supporting and challenging conditions.

No evidence shall be forced into a purely positive or negative category where reality is mixed.

---

##### Review Dimensions

Accumulated pilot evidence shall be evaluated across the following dimensions.

###### RD-001 – Submission Coverage

Question

Were qualifying future floor reservation requests consistently represented through TP-001?

Failure concern

A qualifying request bypasses the protocol and remains dependent on the old informal pathway.

---

###### RD-002 – Information Sufficiency

Question

Did submitted requests contain sufficient information for accurate Guestplan completion?

Failure concern

Missing information creates ambiguity, guessing, delay, or incorrect reservation entry.

---

###### RD-003 – Accountability Continuity

Question

Was one identifiable actor or role responsible for the next required action at every relevant stage?

Failure concern

Responsibility becomes assumed, shared ambiguously, or organizationally invisible.

---

###### RD-004 – Accountability Transfer

Question

Where responsibility transferred, was acceptance explicit and inspectable?

Failure concern

Information is sent but nobody has clearly accepted responsibility for completion.

---

###### RD-005 – Verified Guestplan Completion

Question

Did Completed correspond to actual Guestplan entry and verification?

Failure concern

A request is treated as complete without sufficient evidence that Guestplan accurately represents the accepted commitment.

---

###### RD-006 – Unresolved-State Visibility

Question

Did unresolved requests remain retrievable and distinguishable from completed requests?

Failure concern

A request becomes buried, forgotten, misclassified, or invisible within WhatsApp activity.

---

###### RD-007 – Pre-Service Detection

Question

Were unresolved requests identified before operational preparation or guest arrival exposed the failure?

Failure concern

The failure pattern represented by CE-001 recurs.

---

###### RD-008 – Operational Usability

Question

Could actors execute TP-001 during normal restaurant operations?

Failure concern

The protocol is theoretically correct but routinely bypassed because it is too slow, complex, disruptive, or cognitively demanding.

---

###### RD-009 – Operational Burden

Question

Did TP-001 introduce acceptable or disproportionate additional work?

Failure concern

The mechanism closes one loop by creating unsustainable manual administration elsewhere.

---

###### RD-010 – Message Visibility

Question

Did the designated WhatsApp environment preserve sufficient visibility of unresolved requests?

Failure concern

Unrelated conversation causes reservation requests to become difficult to find or omitted from review.

---

###### RD-011 – Actor Variation

Question

Does the mechanism remain viable across different participating actors?

Failure concern

Success depends on one particularly attentive Manager, Supervisor, Assistant Manager, or Owner rather than the organizational mechanism itself.

---

###### RD-012 – Operating-Condition Variation

Question

Does the mechanism remain viable across materially different operating conditions?

Relevant conditions may include:

- weekdays,
- weekends,
- quiet service periods,
- busy service periods,
- immediate acceptance by a Completion Actor,
- delayed accountability acceptance,
- incomplete information,
- and other exceptions.

Failure concern

The mechanism succeeds only under favorable conditions.

---

##### Evidence Sufficiency Principle

No fixed number of successful events shall automatically establish transformation success.

The initial pilot target remains:

- at least 10 qualifying future floor reservation requests,

or:

- all qualifying requests during the initial 14-day pilot period where fewer than 10 occur.

However, evidence sufficiency depends on:

- diversity of operating conditions,
- actor variation,
- presence or absence of challenge evidence,
- repeated execution,
- protocol compliance,
- observed burden,
- and whether additional evidence is likely to materially change the engineering conclusion.

Ten identical successful events under one favorable condition may provide less confidence than fewer events covering materially different conditions.

---

##### Critical Failure Principle

Not every deviation has equal engineering significance.

A minor formatting deviation shall not automatically invalidate TP-001.

A failure that reproduces the core condition represented by CE-001 shall receive substantially greater weight.

Critical concern exists where:

```text
Guest commitment accepted
        ↓
Request transferred or recorded
        ↓
Completion accountability becomes unclear
        ↓
Guestplan completion remains unresolved
        ↓
Request becomes operationally invisible
        ↓
Failure discovered through guest arrival or late operational discovery
