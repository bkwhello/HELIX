### OC-001 – Multi-Path Reservation Coordination Across Operational Representations

Source Evidence

- E-001 – Multiple Reservation Entry Pathways
- E-002 – Transfer from Guestplan Reservation Records to the Physical Floor Reservation Plan
- E-003 – Manual Deferred Reservation Transfer Without Closed-Loop Confirmation
- E-004 – Weekend Reconciliation Across Known Reservation Entries

Related Work Objects

- WO-001 – Reservation Record
- WO-002 – Floor Reservation Plan

Related Challenge Evidence

- CE-001 – Deferred Manual Entry Without Closed-Loop Confirmation

Claim Status

Established within observed case conditions.

----

#### Organizational Claim

Konnichiwa's reservation operation coordinates guest commitments through multiple reservation entry pathways and multiple operational representations, with Guestplan serving as a principal consolidation point for future reservation information and the physical Floor Reservation Plan serving as an operational representation for service preparation and same-day reservation handling.


#### Evidential Basis

E-001 establishes that reservation-related information and guest-arrival commitments enter the organization through multiple distinct pathways.

E-002 establishes an operational relationship between reservation information represented in Guestplan and the physical Floor Reservation Plan, while also establishing that same-day walk-ins may enter the Floor Reservation Plan directly.

E-003 establishes that at least one future-reservation pathway involves manual deferred transfer without normal closed-loop confirmation and that an actual missed Guestplan entry has been reported.

E-004 establishes that, during weekends, known reservation entries are manually reconciled against Guestplan, Guestplan is updated where necessary, and the physical Floor Reservation Plan is subsequently updated.

Together, this evidence establishes a reservation operation in which guest commitments are coordinated across multiple entry pathways and operational representations rather than through a single universal information path.


#### Falsification Tests

The following stronger interpretations were tested and rejected or remain unestablished:

1. **Guestplan is the universal single source of truth for every reservation-related event.**

Rejected.

Same-day walk-ins may enter the physical Floor Reservation Plan directly, and a reported future reservation failed to reach Guestplan.

2. **Every future reservation reliably reaches Guestplan.**

Rejected.

E-003 establishes a reported actual occurrence in which reservation information remained in the group app without Guestplan entry.

3. **The physical Floor Reservation Plan is merely an identical copy of Guestplan.**

Rejected.

Same-day walk-ins may be added directly to the Floor Reservation Plan.

4. **The reservation operation is organizationally fragmented.**

Not established.

The existence of multiple pathways and representations does not itself establish fragmentation.

5. **The reservation operation is unreliable or defective.**

Not established.

No Organizational Diagnosis has yet been performed.


#### Boundaries

OC-001 establishes the organizational structure of reservation coordination within the observed conditions of EC-001.

It does not establish:

- universal completeness of Guestplan,
- universal completeness of the Floor Reservation Plan,
- reliability or unreliability of the reservation operation,
- effectiveness of weekend reconciliation,
- frequency of reservation failures,
- a design defect,
- or a required organizational intervention.


#### Contradictory Evidence

None currently recorded that contradicts the bounded Organizational Claim.

CE-001 and E-003 do not contradict OC-001. They limit any stronger interpretation that Guestplan universally contains all reservation commitments.


#### Claim Conclusion

OC-001 is sufficiently supported by E-001 through E-004 for use in subsequent Organizational Understanding within the observed conditions of EC-001.

The claim remains bounded by the limitations and challenge evidence preserved within this Engineering Case.

No Organizational Diagnosis established.
