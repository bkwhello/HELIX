### OC-002 – Coexisting Manual Transfer and Reconciliation Mechanisms

Source Evidence

- E-003 – Manual Deferred Reservation Transfer Without Closed-Loop Confirmation
- E-004 – Weekend Reconciliation Across Known Reservation Entries

Supporting Evidence

- E-001 – Multiple Reservation Entry Pathways
- E-002 – Transfer from Guestplan Reservation Records to the Physical Floor Reservation Plan

Related Challenge Evidence

- CE-001 – Deferred Manual Entry Without Closed-Loop Confirmation

Claim Status

Established within observed case conditions.

----

#### Organizational Claim

The reservation operation contains two distinct organizational mechanisms acting on the same reservation information environment:

1. a manual deferred-transfer pathway through which future reservation information may remain outside Guestplan; and
2. a weekend reconciliation practice that reviews known reservation entries, including the same WhatsApp group, against Guestplan.

The evidence establishes the coexistence and informational relationship of these mechanisms.

It does not establish that the weekend reconciliation practice was intentionally designed to address the manual transfer pathway, that it reliably detects omissions, or that it eliminates the failure represented by CE-001.


#### Evidential Basis

E-003 establishes a manual future-reservation transfer pathway in which reservation information may be recorded by a Supervisor, transmitted through the WhatsApp group to the Manager, and entered into Guestplan immediately or after a delay.

E-003 also establishes a reported actual occurrence in which reservation information remained in the group app without being entered into Guestplan and was discovered only when the guest arrived.

E-004 establishes a weekend reconciliation practice in which known reservation entries, explicitly including the WhatsApp group, are reviewed against Guestplan.

Where necessary, Guestplan is updated, after which the physical Floor Reservation Plan is updated.

The same WhatsApp group therefore participates in both:

- the manual deferred-transfer pathway, and
- the weekend reconciliation practice.

This establishes an organizational relationship between the two mechanisms without establishing causal intent or guaranteed effectiveness.

#### Falsification Tests

The following stronger interpretations were tested and rejected or remain unestablished:

1. **Weekend reconciliation eliminates the manual transfer failure pathway.**

Not established.

CE-001 remains open.

2. **Weekend reconciliation reliably detects every missing WhatsApp reservation.**

Not established.

No universal detection effectiveness has been demonstrated.

3. **Weekend reconciliation was intentionally designed to mitigate CE-001.**

Not established.

The origin and design intent of the reconciliation practice have not been established.

4. **The manual transfer pathway frequently fails.**

Not established.

Frequency of occurrence has not been determined.

5. **The coexistence of these mechanisms proves organizational dysfunction.**

Rejected.

The existence of multiple mechanisms does not itself establish dysfunction or a diagnosis.

6. **OC-002 is merely a repetition of E-003 and E-004.**

Rejected.

OC-002 establishes the higher-order organizational relationship that both mechanisms act upon the same reservation information environment and explicitly intersect through the WhatsApp group and Guestplan.


#### Boundaries

OC-002 establishes:

- the existence of a manual deferred-transfer pathway,
- the existence of a weekend reconciliation practice,
- the participation of the WhatsApp group in both mechanisms,
- the relationship of both mechanisms to Guestplan.

OC-002 does not establish:

- that weekend reconciliation was designed as a response to CE-001,
- that reconciliation guarantees detection of missing reservations,
- that reconciliation closes CE-001,
- that the manual pathway fails frequently,
- that Guestplan is universally complete,
- that the organization is dysfunctional,
- or that a specific intervention is required.


#### Contradictory Evidence

None currently recorded that contradicts the bounded Organizational Claim.

The reported actual failure represented by CE-001 does not contradict the existence of weekend reconciliation.

Instead, it prevents a stronger conclusion that reconciliation guarantees successful detection or elimination of the manual-transfer failure pathway.


#### Claim Conclusion

OC-002 is sufficiently supported by E-003 and E-004, with supporting context from E-001 and E-002, for use in subsequent Organizational Understanding within the observed conditions of EC-001.

The claim establishes coexistence and informational relationship without asserting causal intent, guaranteed effectiveness, mitigation, control adequacy, or diagnosis.
