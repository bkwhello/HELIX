### O-004 – Deferred Manual Reservation Entry

Source

Direct account from Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

Observation Status

Recorded – Not Yet Evidentially Validated

----

#### Raw Observation

When a guest makes a future reservation request directly on the restaurant floor:

1. The Supervisor records the reservation information.
2. The Supervisor commonly records only the guest's name and phone number.
3. The written information is photographed.
4. The photograph is sent to the Manager through WhatsApp.
5. The Manager is expected to enter the reservation into Guestplan.
6. The Manager may enter the reservation later because the request is often received during the Manager's private time.
7. The Manager does not normally confirm to the Supervisor that the reservation has been entered into Guestplan.
8. It is possible for the Manager to forget to enter the reservation.


#### Normal Observed Flow

Guest requests future reservation
        ↓
Supervisor records reservation information
        ↓
Written information is photographed
        ↓
Photo is sent to Manager through WhatsApp
        ↓
Manager receives reservation request
        ↓
Immediate entry OR deferred entry
        ↓
Manager enters reservation into Guestplan
        ↓
No confirmation normally returned to Supervisor


#### Reported Failure Possibility

Guest requests future reservation
        ↓
Supervisor records information
        ↓
Photo sent through WhatsApp
        ↓
Manager receives request
        ↓
Entry deferred
        ↓
Manager may forget to enter reservation
        ↓
Reservation may remain absent from Guestplan

This pathway was initially recorded as a reported failure possibility.

A reported actual occurrence was subsequently established through O-005.

Frequency has not yet been established.

Full operational consequences have not yet been established.


#### Channel-Dependent Information Capture

Website reservation information may include:

- name,
- phone number,
- date,
- time,
- dining area,
- number of persons,
- allergy information,
- other relevant information.

Future reservation requests made directly on the restaurant floor may initially record only:

- name,
- phone number.

The exact subsequent process by which additional reservation details are obtained, inferred, or added has not yet been established.

No claim has been made regarding whether this difference is operationally appropriate or problematic.
