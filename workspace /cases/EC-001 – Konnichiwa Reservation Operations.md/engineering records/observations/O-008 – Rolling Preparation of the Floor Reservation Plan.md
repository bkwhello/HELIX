### O-008 – Rolling Preparation of the Floor Reservation Plan

Source

Direct account from Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

Observation Status

Recorded During Evidence Stage – Not Yet Evidentially Validated

----

#### Raw Observation

The Floor Reservation Plan is prepared through a rolling operational process.

Reservations for the current day are checked.

Reservations for the following day are also reviewed in advance.

When operational time is available, reservations for additional future service days may also be reviewed and transferred to their respective Floor Reservation Plans.

This advance preparation does not normally extend beyond three days ahead.

There is no fixed requirement that a Floor Reservation Plan must be created exactly three days before the service date.


#### Observed Preparation Priority

The current observed preparation sequence is:

Today
        ↓
Reservations checked as operational priority
        ↓
Floor Reservation Plan reviewed or updated

Tomorrow
        ↓
Reservations reviewed proactively
        ↓
Floor Reservation Plan prepared or updated

Additional available time
        ↓
Later service days may also be reviewed
        ↓
Preparation may extend up to three days ahead

WO-002 – Floor Reservation Plan

The Floor Reservation Plan receives future reservation information from Guestplan through a rolling preparation process.

This relationship does not establish that all information on the Floor Reservation Plan originates from Guestplan.

Same-day walk-ins may be added directly to the Floor Reservation Plan.
