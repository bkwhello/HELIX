### O-006 – Weekend Reservation Reconciliation

Source

Direct account from Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

Observation Status

Recorded – Not Yet Evidentially Validated

----

#### Raw Observation

During weekends, reservation information from all known reservation entries is reviewed.

This review explicitly includes the WhatsApp group used to communicate future reservation requests made directly on the restaurant floor.

Reservation information from the known entries is compared against Guestplan.

Where necessary, Guestplan is updated.

After Guestplan has been reviewed and updated, the physical Floor Reservation Plan is updated.

Same-day walk-ins are handled differently and may be added directly to the physical Floor Reservation Plan.
