### O-009 – Telephone Reservation Handling and Deferred Modification Transfer

Source

Direct account from Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

Observation Status

Recorded – Not Yet Evidentially Validated

Context

Observed during execution of the TP-001 bounded transformation pilot.

---

#### Raw Observation

Two telephone reservation pathways have been reported.

##### Path A – New Telephone Reservation for Tomorrow

A guest called Konnichiwa and requested a new reservation for the following day.

The reservation was entered directly into Guestplan.

Observed flow:

Guest calls Konnichiwa
        ↓
Requests new reservation for tomorrow
        ↓
Reservation entered directly into Guestplan
        ↓
Guestplan becomes immediate reservation record

No deferred manual transfer through WhatsApp was reported for this event.

No operational problem was reported.

---

##### Path B – Telephone Request to Change an Existing Reservation

A guest called Konnichiwa and requested a change to an existing reservation.

The Supervisor received the telephone call.

The Supervisor:

1. wrote down the requested reservation change,
2. photographed the written information,
3. sent the photograph through WhatsApp.

The Manager received the transferred information.

The Manager:

1. changed the reservation in Guestplan,
2. posted a "done" message in the WhatsApp group.

Observed flow:

Guest calls Konnichiwa
        ↓
Supervisor receives modification request
        ↓
Supervisor writes down requested change
        ↓
Written information is photographed
        ↓
Photo sent through WhatsApp
        ↓
Manager receives information
        ↓
Manager changes reservation in Guestplan
        ↓
Manager posts "done" in WhatsApp group

---

#### Observed Difference Between the Two Paths

The reported new telephone reservation was entered directly into Guestplan.

The reported telephone modification request required a manual transfer between the Supervisor and Manager because the Supervisor received the request and did not directly perform the Guestplan modification.

The two pathways therefore differ in the number of handoffs and completion mechanism:

New telephone reservation:

Direct Guestplan entry
        ↓
Immediate system representation

Telephone modification received by Supervisor:

Manual capture
        ↓
Photograph
        ↓
WhatsApp transfer
        ↓
Manager action
        ↓
Guestplan modification
        ↓
"Done" response

---

#### Relationship to Existing Case Evidence

O-006 exposes a pathway structurally related to the manual future-reservation transfer pathway diagnosed in OD-001.

Shared characteristics include:

- an organizational commitment or requested change is received by a floor-level actor,
- the actor does not directly perform the required Guestplan action,
- information is manually captured,
- information is transferred through WhatsApp,
- another actor performs the Guestplan action.

However, the modification event also differs materially from OD-001:

- the Guestplan reservation already exists,
- the requested organizational action is modification rather than initial reservation creation,
- the existing reservation may contain information that becomes outdated while modification remains pending,
- TP-001 explicitly excludes reservation modifications outside the pilot.

No conclusion is established that OD-001 automatically applies to this pathway.

No expansion of DES-001 or TP-001 is established from this observation alone.

---

#### Completion Communication Observation

In the reported modification event, the Manager posted:

"done"

in the WhatsApp group after changing the reservation in Guestplan.

This provides reported completion communication.

However, the current observation does not establish:

- whether "done" was posted as a direct reply to the original modification request,
- whether the meaning of "done" was unambiguous to all relevant actors,
- whether the Supervisor saw the completion message,
- whether the changed Guestplan details were separately verified,
- whether this practice occurs consistently across modification requests.

---

#### Unknowns Introduced by O-006

The following remain unresolved:

- Who normally answers telephone reservation calls?
- Which actors enter new telephone reservations directly into Guestplan?
- What happens when a Supervisor receives a new reservation request rather than a modification request?
- Does the Supervisor have Guestplan access?
- Are all telephone modification requests transferred through handwritten notes, photographs, and WhatsApp?
- Is the original reservation always correctly identified in the transferred information?
- How quickly are modification requests normally processed?
- Can a modification request remain unresolved?
- Can a guest arrive before a requested modification has been entered into Guestplan?
- Has a telephone modification ever been forgotten or lost?
- Is "done" consistently used to communicate completion?
- Is "done" linked to the original request?
- Are cancellations handled through the same pathway?
- Are allergy changes handled through this pathway?
- Are changes to date, time, guest count, dining area, and notes handled identically?
