### O-003 – Reservation Access and Floor-Level Handling

Source

Direct account from Engineering Lead and organizational actor.

Observation Status

Recorded – Not Yet Evidentially Validated

----

#### Raw Observation

Access to Guestplan is held by:

- Owner
- Manager
- Assistant Manager

These actors can:

- view reservations,
- create reservations,
- change reservations.

When none of these actors is present, a Supervisor may contact the Manager through WhatsApp and request an operational update related to reservation handling or the floor reservation plan.

Walk-in guests are added directly to the floor reservation plan.

When a guest makes a reservation request on the restaurant floor for a future date, the reservation information is:

1. written down,
2. photographed,
3. sent through WhatsApp to the Manager.

The Manager subsequently enters the reservation into Guestplan.

Entry may occur immediately or later.

#### Unknowns Introduced by O-003

The following remain unresolved:

- Does the Supervisor have any direct access to Guestplan?
- Can other employees access Guestplan?
- Can other employees view reservations without having Guestplan credentials?
- Who is responsible for creating the floor reservation plan?
- Is the floor reservation plan physical or digital?
- Where is the floor reservation plan maintained?
- When the Supervisor requests an operational update through WhatsApp, what exactly is the Manager expected to update?
- How quickly are future reservation requests normally entered into Guestplan?
- What happens to the original handwritten reservation information?
- Can reservation information exist temporarily only in handwritten form or WhatsApp?
- How are walk-ins reflected in Guestplan, if at all?

The following questions have been answered by later observation:

- The Manager enters future reservation requests into Guestplan.
- Entry may be immediate or deferred.
- The Manager does not normally confirm completion to the Supervisor.
