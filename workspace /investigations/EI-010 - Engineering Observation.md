# EI-010 – Engineering Observation

Status: Open

Reason

During the investigation of Engineering Evidence it became clear that the definition of an Observation underpins the entire HELIX Engineering Discipline.

Rather than prematurely defining Observation, this investigation has been intentionally reopened.

Current Question

What constitutes an Engineering Observation?

Current Findings

- An Observation is not an explanation.
- An Observation is not an inference.
- An Observation is not architectural knowledge.
- Observations appear to be the atomic unit from which engineering evidence is constructed.

Open Questions

- What makes an Observation objective?
- What metadata must accompany an Observation?
- Can two observers produce different observations of the same phenomenon?
- Is an Observation already a Claim?
- Can an Observation exist without context?

Status

Investigation ongoing.
