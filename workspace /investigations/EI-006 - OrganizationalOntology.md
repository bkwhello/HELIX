# EI-006 – Organizational Ontology

Status: Discovery

Phase: II – Organizational Ontology

Engineering Domain: Organizational Ontology

---

## Objective

Discover the minimum set of concepts required to describe every organization.

---

## Core Question

What fundamentally exists inside every organization?

---

## Investigation

The following candidate categories were investigated:

- Purpose
- Actors
- Organizational Objects
- Capabilities
- Modes of Work
- Relationships

Multiple additional candidates were evaluated and rejected, including:

- Process
- Workflow
- Department
- Information
- Software

These were determined to be either implementations or specializations.

---

## Leading Hypothesis

Every organization consists of a small number of irreducible organizational concepts.

Candidate concepts include:

- Purpose
- Organizational Objects
- Actors
- Capabilities
- Relationships
- Modes of Work

---

## Architectural Impact

Shifted HELIX from Capability Engineering toward Organizational Ontology.

Changed the central engineering question from:

"What capability comes next?"

to

"What fundamentally exists in every organization?"

---

## Architectural Consequences

Initiated investigation into Organizational Reference Modeling.

Introduced Organizational Ontology as a candidate architectural layer above Capabilities.

---

## Supersedes

Capability-first engineering roadmap.
