# EI-005 – Organizational Actor

Status: Discovery

Phase: II – Organizational Ontology

Engineering Domain: Organizational Ontology

---

## Objective

Discover the fundamental organizational phenomenon responsible for performing organizational work.

---

## Core Question

What is an Organizational Actor?

---

## Investigation

Initial assumptions included:

- Human
- Employee
- AI Agent
- Team
- Department
- Robot

These proved to be implementations rather than the underlying organizational phenomenon.

---

## Observations

Actors:

- perform organizational work
- possess capabilities
- accept organizational responsibility
- act upon Organizational Objects

Actors are independent of implementation.

A human, AI employee, department or external organization may all fulfill the Actor role.

---

## Leading Hypothesis

An Organizational Actor is a bearer of organizational responsibility that applies one or more organizational capabilities to Organizational Objects.

---

## Architectural Impact

Introduced candidate concept:

Organizational Actor

Identified Actors as capability carriers rather than capability owners.

Capabilities belong to the organization and are exercised by Actors.

---

## Architectural Consequences

Expanded the Organizational Ontology.

Prepared the discovery of Organizational Capability.

---

## Supersedes

The assumption that Actors are equivalent to people or employees.
