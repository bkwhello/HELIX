# EI-008 – Organizational Reference Model

Status: Active Investigation

Phase: II – Organizational Ontology

Engineering Domain: Organizational Architecture

---

## Objective

Determine whether all discovered organizational concepts can be integrated into a single coherent reference model.

---

## Core Question

Can every organizational phenomenon be expressed using a minimal Organizational Reference Model?

---

## Investigation

Relationships between the following concepts were investigated:

- Purpose
- Organizational Capability
- Organizational Actor
- Organizational Object
- Knowledge Asset
- Work Object
- Modes of Work
- Relationships

The investigation shifted from discovering concepts to discovering their dependencies.

---

## Leading Hypothesis

Organizations can be represented as a dependency network rather than a hierarchy.

Candidate dependency model:

Purpose

↓

Capabilities

↓

Actors

↓

Organizational Objects

↓

Outcomes

Knowledge Assets guide multiple parts of the system.

Relationships connect organizational concepts.

Modes of Work describe organizational transformation.

---

## Architectural Impact

Introduced the concept of a HELIX Organizational Reference Model.

Identified the distinction between:

- Ontology
- Architecture
- Capability Engineering

---

## Architectural Consequences

The Reference Model became the candidate architectural blueprint for all future HELIX capabilities.

Future standards are expected to conform to this model.

---

## Supersedes

Linear capability engineering.
