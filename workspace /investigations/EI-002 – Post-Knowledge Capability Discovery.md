# EI-002 – Post-Knowledge Capability Discovery

Status: Discovery
Phase: Organizational Ontology

---

## Objective

Determine what fundamental organizational problems remain after the Organizational Knowledge Capability has been completed.

The purpose of this investigation is to avoid assuming the next capability and instead discover it through first principles.

---

## Core Question

What organizational work cannot be solved by the Organizational Knowledge Capability alone?

---

## Observation

The Knowledge Capability successfully answers:

> What does the organization know?

It does not answer:

- What knowledge is relevant?
- What should happen now?
- How should conflicting knowledge be resolved?
- How should work be performed?
- How should outcomes be improved?

---

## Initial Candidate Areas

- Retrieval
- Context
- Reasoning
- Decisioning
- Planning
- Coordination
- Communication
- Execution
- Evaluation
- Learning

---

## Major Discovery

The original assumption that "Behavior" was the next capability could not be validated.

Instead, multiple organizational problems were identified between Knowledge and Execution.

---

## Architectural Outcome

The roadmap changed from:

Knowledge
↓
Behavior
↓
Execution

to an investigation of the complete post-knowledge landscape.
