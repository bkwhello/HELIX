AD-014 – Operational Form of the Engineering Case

## Validation Summary

The candidate operational form was challenged through:

- Single-document testing
- Fixed folder structure testing
- Fully adaptive structure testing
- Fixed semantics with adaptive storage testing
- Partial lifecycle testing
- Challenge evidence reduction
- Narrative traceability testing
- Protocol removal testing
- Mid-case restructuring testing
- Minimum valid case record testing
- Technology dependency testing
- Irreducibility testing
- Falsification attempts

The investigation established that:

- The operational form of an Engineering Case shall not be larger than required to preserve its earned engineering responsibilities.
- A single-document form may be sufficient for small Engineering Cases but cannot be assumed sufficient for all cases.
- A fixed physical structure cannot be universally mandatory.
- Unrestricted physical representation would weaken inspectability, traceability, and cross-case comparison.
- Engineering Case semantics must remain invariant while physical representation may adapt to case complexity.
- An Engineering Case need not instantiate every HELIX lifecycle stage.
- Lifecycle scope and termination point must remain explicit.
- Supporting, contradictory, uncertain, unexplained, and failure evidence must remain explicitly distinguishable.
- Traceability is mandatory, but its physical representation may scale with case complexity.
- A minimal execution protocol is necessary to preserve the integrity of evidence production.
- Physical representation may evolve during execution provided case identity, evidence integrity, and traceability remain preserved.
- The operational form must remain technology-independent at the semantic level.

No stronger competing explanation survived.

## Final Discovery

The operational form of an Engineering Case consists of mandatory semantic invariants and a minimal execution protocol, expressed through an adaptive physical representation proportional to case complexity.

Every Engineering Case shall preserve:

- Case Identity
- Purpose or Engineering Question
- Explicit Boundaries
- Observed Conditions
- Lifecycle Scope
- Engineering Records
- Challenge Evidence
- Traceability
- Explicit Status and Termination

The physical representation of an Engineering Case may adapt according to case complexity.

A small Engineering Case may be represented by a single document.

A larger Engineering Case may require multiple artifacts or a structured container.

The physical representation may evolve during execution provided:

- case identity remains stable,
- evidence integrity is preserved,
- traceability is preserved,
- historical meaning is not rewritten.

Engineering Case semantics are mandatory.

Physical representation is adaptive.

The operational form shall remain technology-independent at the semantic level.

## Minimal Execution Protocol

An Engineering Case shall, at minimum:

1. Establish case identity.
2. Define the purpose or Engineering Question.
3. Establish explicit boundaries.
4. Record observed conditions.
5. Declare lifecycle scope.
6. Apply the relevant HELIX Engineering Lifecycle stages.
7. Preserve engineering records as they emerge.
8. Preserve supporting, contradictory, uncertain, unexplained, and failure evidence.
9. Maintain traceability across the applied lifecycle.
10. Close with explicit case status and termination point.

The protocol shall preserve engineering integrity without introducing unnecessary procedural complexity.

## Outcome

The investigation successfully established the operational form required to execute and preserve an Engineering Case.

The evidence supports an adaptive operational model consisting of:

- mandatory semantic invariants,
- a minimal execution protocol,
- an adaptive physical representation proportional to case complexity.

The investigation rejected both:

- one universally mandatory physical structure,
- unrestricted physical representation.

The operational form preserves rigor without requiring unnecessary bureaucracy.

This investigation is closed and transitions to Architectural Discovery.
