# EI-007 – Organizational Capability

Status: Discovery

Phase: II – Organizational Ontology

Engineering Domain: Organizational Ontology

---

## Objective

Determine the fundamental nature of Organizational Capability.

---

## Core Question

What is a Capability?

---

## Investigation

Capabilities were compared against:

- Skills
- Departments
- Software
- Procedures
- Employees

None proved fundamental.

Instead, capabilities appeared to belong to the organization itself.

---

## Observations

Capabilities:

- persist when Actors change
- enable organizational work
- are exercised by Actors
- exist independently from execution

Capabilities represent organizational ability rather than organizational action.

---

## Leading Hypothesis

An Organizational Capability is a governed organizational ability that enables Actors to transform Organizational Objects in pursuit of Organizational Purpose.

---

## Architectural Impact

Separated Capabilities from Actors.

Confirmed that Capabilities belong to the organization rather than individuals.

---

## Architectural Consequences

Strengthened the Organizational Ontology.

Prepared the Organizational Reference Model.

---

## Supersedes

The assumption that capabilities belong to people or departments.
