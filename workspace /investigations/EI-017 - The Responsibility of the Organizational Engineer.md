## Final Discovery

The primary responsibility of the Organizational Engineer is to establish justified, evidence-based claims about organizational reality.

These claims provide the foundation upon which organizational understanding, diagnosis, design, and transformation are built.

The engineer does not create organizational reality.

The engineer establishes justified claims about that reality.
