# AD-006 – Responsibility of the Organizational Engineer

Status

Accepted

Origin

EI-017 – Responsibility of the Organizational Engineer

---

# Discovery

The primary responsibility of the Organizational Engineer is to establish justified, evidence-based claims about organizational reality.

These claims collectively enable organizational understanding.

---

# Responsibility

The Organizational Engineer

- establishes justified claims
- grounds claims in evidence
- makes organizational reality inspectable
- enables legitimate diagnosis
- enables evidence-based organizational design
- enables evidence-based organizational transformation

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through

- Improvement comparison
- Problem-solving comparison
- Consulting comparison
- Design comparison
- Understanding comparison
- Engineering discipline comparison
- Falsification
- Dependency testing

---

# Architectural Impact

Establishes the constitutional responsibility of the Organizational Engineer.

Serves as the foundation for the Organizational Engineering Process.
