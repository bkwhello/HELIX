# AD-007 – Organizational Claim

Status

Accepted

Origin

EI-018 – Organizational Claim

---

# Discovery

An Organizational Claim is a justified engineering proposition about organizational reality supported by evidence and open to inspection, validation, refutation, and refinement.

Claims represent the smallest engineering artifact that carries organizational meaning.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Claims

- externalize engineering reasoning
- connect evidence with understanding
- enable inspection
- enable validation
- enable reproducible engineering

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through

- Observation comparison
- Evidence comparison
- Conclusion comparison
- Understanding comparison
- Diagnosis comparison
- AI engineering testing
- Dependency testing
- Falsification

---

# Architectural Impact

Establishes the Organizational Claim as the atomic engineering artifact of HELIX.

Serves as the foundation for Organizational Understanding, Diagnosis, Design, and Transformation.
