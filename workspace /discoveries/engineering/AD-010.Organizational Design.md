# Discovery

Organizational Design is the justified engineering specification of an intended future organizational state constrained by Organizational Diagnosis.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Design

- specifies the intended future organizational state
- translates diagnosis into a future architecture
- constrains organizational transformation
- provides the engineering blueprint for intervention

- Recommendation comparison

- Transformation comparison
- Multiple design testing
- Constraint testing
- AI engineering comparison
- Falsification

# Architectural Impact

Introduces Organizational Design as the engineering bridge between Organizational Diagnosis and Organizational Transformation.
