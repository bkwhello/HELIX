# AD-009 – Organizational Diagnosis

Status

Accepted

Discovery Domain

Engineering

Origin

EI-020 – Organizational Diagnosis

---

# Discovery

Organizational Diagnosis is the justified engineering assessment of organizational condition derived from Organizational Understanding.

Diagnosis determines the current organizational condition without prescribing organizational solutions.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Diagnosis

- assesses organizational condition
- evaluates organizational health
- identifies organizational deficiencies
- identifies organizational strengths
- establishes the engineering basis for Organizational Design

---

# Principles

Diagnosis shall:

- be evidence-based
- be traceable to Organizational Understanding
- remain open to validation
- remain open to refinement
- precede Organizational Design

Diagnosis shall not prescribe organizational interventions.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through

- Diagnosis removal testing
- Understanding substitution
- Claim substitution
- Recommendation substitution
- Medicine comparison
- AI engineering comparison
- Organizational condition testing
- Dependency testing
- Falsification attempts

---

# Architectural Impact

Introduces Organizational Diagnosis as the engineering bridge between Organizational Understanding and Organizational Design.
