# AD-014 – Operational Form of the Engineering Case

Status

Accepted

Discovery Domain

Engineering

Origin

EI-025 – Operational Form of the Engineering Case

---

# Discovery

The operational form of an Engineering Case consists of mandatory semantic invariants and a minimal execution protocol, expressed through an adaptive physical representation proportional to case complexity.

Engineering Case semantics are mandatory.

Physical representation is adaptive.

---

# Responsibility

The operational form of an Engineering Case shall:

- preserve the irreducible responsibilities established by AD-013,
- establish predictable engineering semantics,
- preserve inspectability,
- preserve evidence integrity,
- preserve traceability,
- preserve challenge evidence,
- scale according to case complexity,
- avoid unnecessary procedural or structural complexity,
- remain technology-independent at the semantic level.

---

# Mandatory Semantic Invariants

Every Engineering Case shall preserve:

## Case Identity

Uniquely identifies the bounded Engineering Case.

## Purpose or Engineering Question

States why the case exists and what engineering question it seeks to investigate.

## Explicit Boundaries

Defines the organizational, temporal, and engineering boundaries of the case.

## Observed Conditions

Records the conditions under which case evidence is produced.

## Lifecycle Scope

Declares:

- lifecycle stages in scope,
- lifecycle stages completed,
- termination point,
- reason for termination where relevant.

## Engineering Records

Preserves the engineering artifacts produced by the lifecycle stages within scope.

These may include:

- Observations
- Evidence
- Organizational Claims
- Organizational Understanding
- Organizational Diagnosis
- Organizational Design
- Organizational Transformation

## Challenge Evidence

Explicitly preserves evidence relevant to challenging, qualifying, or refining the Engineering Case and the HELIX Engineering Discipline.

Challenge Evidence may include:

- Supporting Evidence
- Contradictory Evidence
- Uncertainty
- Unexplained Phenomena
- Failure Evidence

Challenge Evidence shall remain explicitly distinguishable according to its evidence state.

A Challenge Evidence record may change classification as additional evidence is established, provided its historical evidence state remains preserved.

For example:

Uncertainty
    ↓
Actual occurrence established
    ↓
Failure Evidence

Such a transition shall not silently overwrite the earlier evidence state.

Case-specific Challenge Evidence records belong within the relevant Engineering Case and shall not be stored within this Architectural Discovery.

## Traceability

Preserves inspectable relationships across the applied HELIX Engineering Lifecycle.

Traceability may be represented through:

- inline references,
- explicit identifiers,
- traceability tables,
- dedicated traceability structures,
- or other suitable mechanisms proportional to case complexity.

## Status and Termination

Explicitly records:

- current case status,
- lifecycle stages completed,
- termination point,
- reason for termination where relevant.

---

# Minimal Execution Protocol

An Engineering Case shall, at minimum:

1. Establish case identity.
2. Define the purpose or Engineering Question.
3. Establish explicit boundaries.
4. Record observed conditions.
5. Declare lifecycle scope.
6. Apply the relevant HELIX Engineering Lifecycle stages.
7. Preserve engineering records as they emerge.
8. Preserve supporting, contradictory, uncertain, unexplained, and failure evidence.
9. Maintain traceability across the applied lifecycle.
10. Close with explicit case status and termination point.

The protocol shall preserve engineering integrity without introducing unnecessary procedural detail.

---

# Adaptive Physical Representation

The physical representation of an Engineering Case may adapt according to case complexity.

A small Engineering Case may use:

EC-XXX.md

A larger Engineering Case may use:

EC-XXX/
├── README.md
├── evidence/
├── claims/
└── diagnosis.md

A complex Engineering Case may use a more extensive structure where required.

No particular physical structure is mandatory merely because it is available.

Physical structure shall be proportional to engineering need.

---

# Evolution During Execution

The physical representation of an Engineering Case may evolve during execution provided:

- case identity remains stable,
- evidence integrity is preserved,
- traceability is preserved,
- historical meaning is not rewritten.

---

# Technology Independence

The operational form of an Engineering Case is defined semantically rather than technologically.

An Engineering Case may be represented using:

- Markdown,
- databases,
- graph structures,
- specialized engineering software,
- AI engineering environments,
- or combinations of these.

Implementation technology shall not redefine the fundamental responsibilities of the Engineering Case.

# Architectural Impact

Operationalizes the Engineering Case established by AD-013.

Introduces:

- mandatory semantic invariants,
- a minimal execution protocol,
- adaptive physical representation,
- technology independence.

Enables Engineering Cases to remain rigorous without imposing unnecessary structural bureaucracy.

Establishes the operational basis required to begin the first real Engineering Case.
