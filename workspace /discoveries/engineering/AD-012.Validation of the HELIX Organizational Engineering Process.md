Status

Discovery Domain

# Discovery

The HELIX Organizational Engineering Process accumulates justified validation confidence through evidence produced by disciplined application to organizational reality under explicitly observed conditions.

Validation is not a binary achievement.

No finite set of successful cases establishes universal validity.

Engineering Cases produce validation evidence; they do not independently validate HELIX.

---

# Responsibility

HELIX validation shall:

- accumulate justified confidence through evidence,
- preserve the explicitly observed conditions of application,
- require inspectable engineering artifacts,
- preserve traceability across the engineering lifecycle,
- permit independent challenge,
- distinguish reproducibility from identical conclusions,
- require explainable divergence where conclusions differ,
- preserve failures and contradictions as engineering evidence,
- enable refinement when evidence challenges the discipline.

---

# Validation Properties

## Inspectability

Engineering artifacts must be available for examination and challenge.

## Traceability

Engineering conclusions must remain traceable through the engineering lifecycle.

## Reproducibility

Independent application should produce sufficiently reproducible results under comparable conditions.

Exact identity of conclusions is not required.

## Explainable Divergence

Differences between engineering conclusions must be explainable through evidence, scope, assumptions, observed conditions, time, or reasoning.

## Contradiction

Evidence that contradicts HELIX shall not be suppressed or dismissed.

Contradiction is evidence about the discipline.

## Failure

Failures shall remain visible and shall be traced backwards through the engineering lifecycle where possible.

## Conditional Confidence

Validation confidence applies only within the conditions for which supporting evidence exists.

- Divergence analysis

---

# Architectural Impact

Establishes the epistemic basis for validating the HELIX Organizational Engineering Process through real-world application.

Establishes that Engineering Cases are evidence-producing mechanisms rather than independent proof of validity.

Provides the foundation for future Engineering Cases, cross-case analysis, independent challenge, contradiction analysis, and method refinement.
