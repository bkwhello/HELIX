# AD-011 – Organizational Transformation

Status

Accepted

Discovery Domain

Engineering

Origin

EI-022 – Organizational Transformation

---

# Discovery

Organizational Transformation is the justified engineering realization of an intended organizational state specified by Organizational Design.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Transformation

- realizes Organizational Design
- changes operational organizational reality
- preserves engineering traceability
- establishes the new organizational state

---

# Principles

Transformation shall

- realize justified Organizational Design
- remain traceable to Organizational Diagnosis
- preserve engineering intent
- produce observable organizational change

Transformation shall not redesign the organization during realization.

---

- Design comparison
- Project Management comparison
- Implementation comparison
- AI engineering comparison
- Organizational reality testing
- Dependency testing
- Falsification
