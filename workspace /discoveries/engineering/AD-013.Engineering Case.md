# AD-013 – Engineering Case

Status

Accepted

Discovery Domain

Engineering

Origin

EI-024 – The Engineering Case

---

# Discovery

An Engineering Case is a bounded and disciplined application of the HELIX Organizational Engineering Process to organizational reality that preserves inspectable, traceable, and condition-aware evidence, including supporting, contradictory, uncertain, unexplained, and failure evidence, for subsequent validation and refinement of the discipline.

The Engineering Case is fundamentally the bounded application itself.

It is not merely a document, container, protocol, client engagement, demonstration, or validation conclusion.

---

# Responsibility

An Engineering Case shall:

- bound an application of HELIX to organizational reality,
- preserve the explicitly observed conditions of application,
- preserve the lifecycle scope being tested,
- produce inspectable engineering evidence,
- preserve bidirectional traceability,
- preserve supporting evidence,
- preserve contradictory evidence,
- preserve uncertainty,
- preserve unexplained phenomena,
- preserve failure evidence,
- remain capable of supporting or challenging confidence in HELIX.

---

# Boundaries

An Engineering Case shall explicitly establish relevant boundaries, including:

- organizational scope,
- temporal scope,
- engineering scope,
- observed conditions.

The meaning of case evidence depends upon the boundaries and conditions under which that evidence was produced.

---

# Lifecycle Scope

An Engineering Case may cover the complete HELIX Organizational Engineering Lifecycle or a bounded subset of it.

The case shall explicitly declare:

- lifecycle stages in scope,
- lifecycle stages completed,
- termination point,
- reason for termination where relevant.

Successful completion of the intended lifecycle scope is not required for a case to produce valid engineering evidence.

---

# Evidence

An Engineering Case may produce:

- supporting evidence,
- contradictory evidence,
- uncertain evidence,
- unexplained observations,
- failure evidence.

Evidence against HELIX shall not be suppressed merely because it challenges the discipline.

Contradiction and failure are legitimate engineering evidence.

---

# Traceability

An Engineering Case shall preserve sufficient traceability across the applied HELIX Organizational Engineering Lifecycle.

Forward traceability supports inspection of engineering progression.

Backward traceability supports challenge, contradiction analysis, and failure investigation.

---

# Nature of the Engineering Case

The Engineering Case is fundamentally a bounded application of the HELIX Organizational Engineering Process to organizational reality.

A protocol may govern its execution.

A container may preserve its records.

Artifacts may make its evidence inspectable and traceable.

None of these individually constitutes the Engineering Case itself.

---

# Architectural Impact

Establishes the Engineering Case as the evidence-producing mechanism for disciplined application of HELIX to organizational reality.

Separates:

- application from validation,
- case evidence from validation conclusions,
- Engineering Cases from client projects,
- Engineering Cases from narrative case studies,
- the bounded application itself from its protocol, container, and documentary artifacts.

Provides the foundation for operationalizing Engineering Cases and producing real-world evidence capable of supporting or challenging confidence in HELIX.
