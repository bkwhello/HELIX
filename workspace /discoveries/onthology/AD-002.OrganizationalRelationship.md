# AD-002 – Organizational Relationship

Status: Accepted

Origin

EI-013 – Organizational Relationship

---

# Discovery

An Organizational Relationship is a persistent structural connection between organizational concepts that carries information not contained within the connected concepts themselves.

Relationships explain how organizational concepts become a coherent system rather than isolated elements.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through:

- removal testing
- object reduction testing
- property reduction testing
- interaction comparison
- language independence testing
- AI organization testing
- reference model dependency testing

---

# Architectural Impact

Adds Organizational Relationship as a validated concept in the Organizational Ontology.

Relationship is not reducible to:

- Organizational Object
- Property
- Language
- Interaction

---

# Architectural Role

Organizational Relationships provide structural meaning.

They connect organizational concepts and allow Reference Models to exist.

Without relationships, the ontology becomes a glossary rather than a model.
