# AD-001 – Organizational Purpose

Status: Accepted

Origin

EI-012 – Organizational Purpose

---

# Discovery

Organizational Purpose is the enduring justification for an organization's existence.

Purpose explains why an organization exists and provides the rationale from which capabilities, governance, and strategic direction derive.

Purpose does not govern operational execution; it justifies organizational existence.
