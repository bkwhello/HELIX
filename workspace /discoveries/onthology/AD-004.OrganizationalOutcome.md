# AD-004 – Organizational Outcome

Status

Accepted

Origin

EI-015 – Organizational Outcome

---

# Discovery

Organizational Outcome is the realized organizational effect of work evaluated in relation to Organizational Purpose.

Outcome represents how organizational activity changes organizational reality.

Outcomes may be intended or unintended.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Outcome

- represents realized organizational effects
- demonstrates organizational achievement
- provides evidence of Purpose realization
- enables organizational evaluation

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through

- Dependency testing
- Work Object reduction
- Goal substitution
- Performance substitution
- Change in Reality comparison
- AI organization testing
- Counterexamples
- Falsification
