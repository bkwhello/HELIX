# AD-003 – Organizational Governance

Status

Accepted

Origin

EI-014 – Organizational Governance

---

# Discovery

Organizational Governance is the persistent organizational mechanism that legitimizes decision authority, constrains organizational behavior, and preserves organizational integrity throughout operation and change.

---

# Responsibility

Governance establishes legitimate decision authority.

Governance preserves organizational integrity.

Governance enables controlled organizational evolution.
