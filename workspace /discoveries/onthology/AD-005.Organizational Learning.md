# AD-005 – Organizational Learning

Status

Accepted

Origin

EI-016 – Organizational Learning

---

# Discovery

Organizational Learning is the persistent organizational phenomenon through which previous Organizational Outcomes systematically influence future organizational behavior, structures, governance, or capabilities.

Learning enables organizations to evolve through organizational experience.

---

# Responsibility

Organizational Learning

- explains organizational evolution
- connects Outcomes to future organizational behavior
- enables continuous organizational adaptation
- preserves organizational improvement over time

---

# Supporting Evidence

Validated through

- Learning removal testing
- Governance substitution
- Capability substitution
- Outcome substitution
- Change comparison
- Random adaptation testing
- AI organization testing
- Dependency testing
- Counterexamples
