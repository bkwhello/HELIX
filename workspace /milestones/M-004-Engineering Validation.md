## Current State

M-004 has established:

- the conditions under which validation confidence in HELIX may legitimately accumulate,
- that Engineering Cases produce validation evidence rather than independently validating HELIX,
- the fundamental nature and responsibility of the Engineering Case,
- that an Engineering Case is a bounded application of HELIX to organizational reality,
- that Engineering Cases preserve inspectable, traceable, and condition-aware evidence,
- that contradiction, uncertainty, unexplained phenomena, and failure are legitimate engineering evidence,
- that Engineering Cases require mandatory semantic invariants,
- that Engineering Cases require a minimal execution protocol,
- that physical representation may adapt according to case complexity,
- that the operational form remains technology-independent at the semantic level.

The operational basis required to begin the first real Engineering Case has been established.
