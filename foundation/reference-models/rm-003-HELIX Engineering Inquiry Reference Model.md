# Purpose

Define the canonical conceptual model through which HELIX performs engineering inquiry to develop justified conclusions.

RM-003 defines the concepts, relationships and boundaries governing engineering inquiry.

It does not define engineering procedures, governance authority or knowledge lifecycle management.

---

# Engineering Question

How does HELIX conduct engineering inquiry to develop justified conclusions?

# Core Concepts

## Reality

Operational reality independent of HELIX.

## Observation

A recorded encounter with reality.

## Information

Recorded content.

## Evidence

Information evaluated relative to an engineering question or claim.

## Engineering Question

The governing question defining an inquiry.

## Claim

A proposition capable of support or challenge.

## Finding

A result produced through analysis.

## Understanding

A coherent explanation supported by findings.

## Conclusion

A bounded answer to an engineering question.

## Challenge

A recorded contest to observations, evidence, reasoning or conclusions.

# Conceptual Invariants

## IQ-001

Reality and records remain distinct.

## IQ-002

Information becomes evidence only relative to a defined engineering question or claim.

## IQ-003

Evidence supports or challenges claims.

## IQ-004

Claims are distinct from findings.

## IQ-005

Findings are distinct from conclusions.

## IQ-006

Conclusions remain bounded by scope and evidence.

## IQ-007

Challenges remain preserved.

## IQ-008

Engineering inquiry may legitimately terminate without resolving the governing question.

# Relationship to RM-004

RM-003 ends at justified conclusions.

RM-004 begins when a conclusion is proposed for preservation and reuse as engineering knowledge.

---

# Boundary

RM-003 terminates with the production of justified conclusions.

Knowledge preservation, authority, validation for reuse, lifecycle management and governance belong to RM-004.

---

# Conclusion

RM-003 establishes the canonical Engineering Inquiry model for HELIX.

Its responsibility is limited to how justified conclusions are produced through engineering inquiry.

Management of engineering knowledge is outside the scope of RM-003.
