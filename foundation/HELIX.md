**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Living Foundation Document

# What is HELIX?

HELIX is the world's first Organizational Engineering framework.

It provides the principles, language, standards, and reference architecture for designing, building, operating, and continuously improving AI-native organizations.

HELIX is not an AI framework.

HELIX is not an orchestration platform.

HELIX is not an automation tool.

HELIX is a new engineering discipline.

---

# Our Mission

Engineer organizations that are capable of remembering, learning, adapting, and continuously improving through structured architecture, governed knowledge, explicit behavior, and implementation-independent execution.

---

# Our Vision

Create a future where organizations are engineered with the same rigor, discipline, and reliability that humanity applies to software, aviation, medicine, and civil engineering.

---

# Why HELIX Exists

Organizations have become increasingly complex.

Knowledge is fragmented.

Responsibilities are unclear.

Processes are undocumented.

Experience disappears when people leave.

AI has accelerated execution but has not solved organizational design.

HELIX exists because organizations deserve an engineering discipline of their own.

---

# Organizational Engineering

Organizational Engineering is the discipline of designing, governing, implementing, operating, and continuously improving organizations through structured architecture.

It combines:

- Organizational Design
- Knowledge Management
- Governance
- Systems Thinking
- Artificial Intelligence
- Continuous Learning

into one coherent engineering methodology.

# The Role of AI

Artificial Intelligence is not the organization.

Artificial Intelligence is not the architecture.

Artificial Intelligence is not the knowledge.

AI represents the workforce operating within the organization.

HELIX governs the organization.

AI executes within it.

---

# Organizational Memory

Knowledge belongs to the organization.

Employees consume knowledge.

Knowledge is never owned by individuals.

The organization remembers.

Employees contribute.

HELIX preserves organizational memory independently of technology, people, or AI models.

---

# Organizational Intelligence

HELIX separates organizational intelligence into distinct layers.

Knowledge answers:

> What is true?

Behavior answers:

> How should decisions be made?

Execution answers:

> How is work performed?

Evaluation answers:

> Did we achieve the desired outcome?

Learning answers:

> What should improve?

Improvement answers:

> How does the organization evolve?

Together these layers form the Organizational Intelligence of HELIX.

# HSL

The HELIX Specification Language (HSL) provides the language used to describe organizational architecture.

HSL is implementation-independent.

Any technology may implement HSL.

No implementation owns HSL.

---

# The HELIX Knowledge Network

Knowledge exists as interconnected Knowledge Objects.

Knowledge Objects are:

- uniquely identified
- governed
- versioned
- related
- reusable
- independently managed

Employees reference Knowledge Objects.

They never duplicate organizational knowledge.
