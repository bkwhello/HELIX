# Purpose
---

Engineer a measurable and repeatable organizational capability that improves Konnichiwa's non-branded online visibility and converts that visibility into qualified reservation demand.

The first execution window targets material improvement within 30 days. A top-three search position is a target condition, not a fact, promise, or guaranteed outcome.

## Engineering Question

What organizational conditions, signals, routines, and interventions are required for Konnichiwa to reach and sustain top-three local visibility for selected high-intent search themes, while producing measurable reservation demand?

## Business Outcome

Konnichiwa should become easier to discover by people in and around Utrecht who are actively searching for a premium Japanese dining experience.

Target search themes for the initial window:

1. teppanyaki Utrecht
2. omakase Utrecht
3. Japans restaurant Utrecht

Secondary search theme:

4. sushi Utrecht — target is measurable progress during the first 30 days, not an assured top-three result.
