## Challenge (Role B — Relationship Challenger)

*Independent challenge, 24 July 2026. This challenge is deliberately more skeptical than UR-001–UR-003's, because this candidate was constructed specifically to test the outer boundary of what can be related to OC-002 — a genuine test requires a genuine chance of failure.*

1. **Are both claims independently justified?** Yes — both are Justified Organizational Claims.
2. **Does evidence support the relationship or only the individual claims?** Only the individual claims. "Both figures happen to appear in the same source screenshot set" is a fact about data provenance, not a fact about the organizational conditions the claims describe. No evidence establishes that the naming-volume figure and the engagement-decline figure interact, correlate, or inform one another in any way beyond common ancestry in a single export.
3. **Is the relationship broader than its evidence?** Yes, on reflection — "shared source" is being used to imply the two conditions belong together organizationally, when the only actual connection is administrative (one export, one date, one account). This is broader than what "the same screenshots contained both tables" supports.
4. **Is co-occurrence being represented as dependency?** Risk identified — "coexisting condition" as a type still implies enough proximity that a reader could infer relevance; here there genuinely is none beyond data provenance.
5. **Is contrast being represented as explanation?** Not applicable — no contrast asserted.
6. **Is a current-state screenshot being used as historical evidence?** Not directly — EV-015 is a real 6-month collection, not a current-state screenshot in the E-05/E-06/E-07 sense. Not the failure mode here.
7. **Is Partial evidence being treated as complete?** No.
8. **Does CR-006 contaminate the relationship?** No.
9. **Is OC-007 represented as an operational failure rather than a measurement constraint?** Not applicable.
10. **Does the wording imply a cause?** The Excluded Interpretation section correctly disclaims causation, but the underlying problem is more basic: even a non-causal "these coexist meaningfully" framing is not supported, because nothing about *why* they'd meaningfully coexist (beyond same-file provenance) is offered.
11. **Could the relationship survive if OC-002 remains standalone?** This is the deciding test. If OC-002 remaining standalone is an acceptable, non-deficient outcome (per DD-014 Condition 5), then a relationship this thin should not be preserved merely to avoid that outcome. The relationship does not need to exist for the case to be coherent — its absence changes nothing about what is understood.
12. **Does the relationship add organizational meaning beyond repeating claims?** **No.** This is the controlling failure. Restating "OC-002 and OC-004 come from the same GBP export" adds no synthesis, no new question, and no reader value beyond what each claim's own Traceability line (O-002/O-004 → EV-015) already states. A genuinely useful adjacent observation exists nearby — that GBP discovery-term volume (a different figure than the misspelling volume specifically) remained non-trivial across the same window — but that observation is not actually part of OC-004's own Scope (which is about the naming variant, not discovery-volume trends generally), so asserting it here would exceed OC-004's own boundaries rather than relate to them.

**Outcome: Rejected.** Fails test 12 decisively, with tests 3 and 4 as contributing findings. This is not a failure of the reconstruction process — it is the correct outcome of genuinely testing whether OC-002 relates to anything else, per DD-014's own invitation to test this. **OC-002 is confirmed, through this attempt, to have no relationship among the seven Justified Claims that survives challenge.** It is classified as a Standalone Condition in Phase 3 below, consistent with DD-014 Condition 5, which explicitly permits this outcome.
