## Explicitly Not Integrated

**OC-002 (Sustained Multi-Metric Decline in Google Business Profile Engagement)** and **OC-007 (Reservation Volume Is Measured but Not Attributable to Visibility Channels)** are **not** incorporated into either Organizational Understanding above, and this is a deliberate, recorded decision, not an oversight:

- OC-002 describes a six-month, all-metric decline that this case's evidence base does not currently connect to anything else — not to the search-presence findings in OU-001 (different channel, non-overlapping evidence), not to the technical findings in OU-002 (the decline predates any technical change this case made), and not to OC-007 (no channel attribution exists to test the connection). Forcing OC-002 into either Understanding record would manufacture a coherence the evidence does not support. EM-001 defines Organizational Understanding as "the coherent organization of justified Organizational Claims" — coherence requires an actual, evidenced relationship, not mere co-occurrence within the same case.
- OC-007 describes a structural measurement gap (no channel attribution is currently possible), which is a precondition for testing connections between the other claims and business outcomes, not itself a condition that combines with them into a shared narrative.

Per EM-001 ("Engineering diagnosis shall be based upon Organizational Understanding rather than isolated Organizational Claims"), OC-002 and OC-007 are **not yet eligible for Organizational Diagnosis** on their own — despite OC-002 being, by a wide margin, the single largest and most consequential finding in this case. This is recorded explicitly as a real constraint, not resolved by manufacturing a thin, artificial one-claim "understanding" around it. See decisions/DD-011 for the gate review addressing this directly and for the two paths available to resolve it.
