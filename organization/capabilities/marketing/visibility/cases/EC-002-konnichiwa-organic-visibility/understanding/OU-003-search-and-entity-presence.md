## Understanding Statement

Konnichiwa's non-branded search and local-pack presence is uneven: strong and cross-corroborated for its two flagship dining formats (teppanyaki, omakase), materially weaker for its two broader categories (japans restaurant, sushi). A separate, independently-confirmed entity-naming inconsistency (the "konichiwa" misspelling and related variants) generates real, non-trivial search volume across two platforms. No evidence connects the naming inconsistency to the visibility contrast — both are established facts that coexist without a demonstrated relationship.

## Organizational Significance

For the Marketing Visibility Capability, this Understanding reframes "improve search visibility" from a single, undifferentiated goal into two distinguishable questions with different evidence bases: (1) why do the broader categories underperform relative to the flagship formats, and (2) does the naming inconsistency matter, independent of the visibility contrast. Treating these as one problem risks solving the wrong one; this Understanding keeps them separated for any future prioritization decision.

## What Is Established

- Teppanyaki and omakase have materially stronger non-branded search positions than japans restaurant and sushi, within the stated Search Console window (OC-001).
- Omakase additionally holds a favorable local-pack position at one verified Utrecht point in time (OC-003).
- A real, measurable naming inconsistency exists, confirmed independently on Search Console and GBP (OC-004).
- These three conditions coexist within overlapping but not identical measurement windows in July 2026.

## What Is Not Established

- Why the broader categories underperform (competitive crowding, content, or another cause — a Diagnosis-stage question).
- Whether the naming inconsistency has any effect on visibility, positive or negative.
- Whether the flagship-format strength or the local-pack position is stable over time, seasons, or across other Utrecht locations/devices (OC-003 is one point only).
- Whether correcting the naming inconsistency would change either pattern.
- Any business-outcome consequence of any part of this pattern — constrained by UR-003 (Attribution Constraint), see Constraints below.
