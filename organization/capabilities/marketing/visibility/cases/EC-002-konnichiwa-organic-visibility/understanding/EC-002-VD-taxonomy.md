# EC-002-VD — Visibility Defect Taxonomy
---

Migrated from the original EC-002 case file §17, IDs preserved. This is a **conceptual taxonomy** — a shared vocabulary used to classify findings across observations, evidence, and transformation records. It is not itself a validated Organizational Understanding record per EM-001 (Understanding must emerge from the coherent organization of justified Organizational Claims). It is kept because it is actively referenced by transformation/HV-IR-001.md and design/HV-VCM-001.md.

| ID | Name | Definition | Confirmed instances so far |
|---|---|---|---|
| EC-002-VD-001 | Missing Representation | An important organizational fact or capability is not represented | Sushi-omakase (on-request) was undocumented anywhere online (omakase-pagina-brief.md) |
| EC-002-VD-002 | Contradictory Representation | Two or more sources provide conflicting information | Opening-hours contradictions across AI systems and one search summary (HV-IV-003, HV-IV-004) |
| EC-002-VD-003 | Stale Representation | Published information no longer reflects current reality | Holiday closure notice missing year (HV-IV-004) |
| EC-002-VD-004 | Weak Evidence | A claim exists but lacks sufficient independent or authoritative support | Not yet formally instantiated |
| EC-002-VD-005 | Intent Coverage Gap | A relevant customer intent lacks an adequate knowledge asset or evidence path | Omakase Utrecht (HV-IV-003/004/005) |
| EC-002-VD-006 | Entity Ambiguity | A system cannot clearly identify the organization, service, location, person, or concept | Three name variants: Konnichiwa / Konnichi Wa / Konichiwa (HV-IV-001, HV-IV-003) |
| EC-002-VD-007 | Relationship Ambiguity | A relevant relationship is missing or unclear | Not yet formally instantiated |
