## Headline finding: consistent decline across every metric, 6 months running

| Metric | Feb 2026 (start) | Jul 2026 (latest) | 6-month total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Interacties met Bedrijfsprofiel | ~2,000/mnd | ~700/mnd | 8,109 |
| Profielweergaven (views) | — | — | 68,650 |
| Websiteklikken | ~1,500/mnd | ~400/mnd | 5,410 |
| Opgevraagde routes | ~350/mnd | ~200/mnd | 1,653 |
| Telefoongesprekken | ~110/mnd | ~35/mnd | 418 |
| Menucontent bekeken | ~100/mnd | ~15/mnd | 385 |
| Afspraken (reservation-type actions) | ~55/mnd | ~25/mnd | 243 |

Every single measured GBP metric — profile interactions, website clicks, route requests, phone calls, menu views, and appointments — has declined steadily and continuously since February 2026, roughly halving to two-thirds down by July. This is not a single dip; the trend line is consistently downward across all 6 monthly points in every chart. This predates EC-002 entirely (the case did not exist before 22 July 2026) and predates HV-INT-002 (live 22 July 2026) — none of this can be attributed to any EC-002 intervention.

**Classification: evidence, unexplained.** Per HV-MP-P-006 (No False Attribution), no cause is assumed. Candidate explanations not yet tested: seasonality (but Feb→Jul is not an obvious single-season pattern for a restaurant), a Google Business Profile algorithm/category change, a GBP-side setting change (e.g. altered categories, attributes, or photo activity), declining review velocity, increased competitor GBP investment, or a genuine drop in underlying demand. This is now one of the most consequential open findings in the case — it is a larger, longer-running signal than anything found in the Search Console data (O-001) and should be weighed heavily when the case moves to Organizational Understanding/Diagnosis.
