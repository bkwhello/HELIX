- **This count is an aggregate realtime-window observation, not exact per-click deduplication or event-cardinality proof** — consistent with the same caveat already recorded for `phone_click` in O-019. ## D. Evidence boundary — explicitly not claimed

This event proves only: **a qualifying directions/Google Maps link click can be detected by the production measurement chain and received by GA4.** It does **not** establish, and this observation makes no claim toward:
- that Google Maps successfully opened for the visitor;
- that route guidance started;
- that the visitor travelled to or arrived at Konnichiwa;
- that a reservation occurred;
- that a telephone call occurred;
- exact per-click GA4 deduplication (see the Realtime caveat above);
- completion of the broader first-party measurement chain.

## What this does not establish
