# O-019 — Production Validation of `phone_click` Measurement Path
---

Status: **Collected.** Records the completed production validation of the `phone_click` GA4 event, following the same GTM root-cause/reconciliation approach used for `reservation_click` (observations/O-016.md–O-018.md), applied to the telephone-CTA measurement gap.

---

## GTM configuration evidence

- **Container:** `GTM-WXH5P6SN`. **GA4 Measurement ID:** `G-C29ZMF288W`.
- **Published version:** Version 6, named "GA4 phone_click tracking," published 2026-08-27.
- **Trigger added:** `Phone link click` — type Click - Just Links, fires on Some Link Clicks, condition `{{Click URL}} starts with tel:`. Chosen over the `[data-track="phone_click"]`/All-Elements pattern used for `reservation_click` specifically because at least one tracked phone link (footer) has a nested icon element inside the `<a>`, and Just Links' link-resolution behavior handles clicks on nested children without depending on markup attributes.
- **Tag added:** `GA4 - Event - phone_click` — GA4 Event tag, Measurement ID `G-C29ZMF288W`, event name `phone_click`, trigger `Phone link click`, firing once per event.
- **Existing objects confirmed not intentionally changed:** `GA4 - Event - reservation_click`, the paused `GA4 - Event - reserve_click`, and the `Google-tag G-C29ZMF288W` base config tag.

## GTM Preview behavioral evidence

- **Header** (`class="header--call__link"`): click produced Click Text `+31 30 2416388`, Click URL resolved to `tel:+31302416388`; `phone_click` fired successfully.
- **Footer** (`class="footer__phone__link"`): click resolved to Click URL `tel:+31302416388`; `phone_click` fired successfully, exactly once for the observed event; the preceding `gtm.linkClick` event was confirmed.
- **Not proven:** the specific case of a click landing directly on the nested phone icon inside the footer link, as distinct from a click landing on the link generally/its text. The available evidence describes Click Classes/Click Text/Click URL for the footer link but does not establish icon-specific DOM targeting. This is recorded as an open evidence gap, not inferred as passed.
- **Catering fallback `tel:` links** (page-catering.php, template-catering-eng.php): **untested**. Not reachable safely in this validation round; not promoted to any verified status.

## `reservation_click` regression / isolation evidence

- A reservation CTA click, retested in the same session, fired `GA4 - Event - reservation_click` and did not fire `phone_click` or the paused `reserve_click`.
- A phone-link click fired `phone_click` and did not fire `reservation_click` or `reserve_click`.
- Confirms event separation between the two mechanisms; no cross-firing observed in either direction.

## GTM Version 6 publication evidence

Version 6 ("GA4 phone_click tracking") was published 2026-08-27, containing exactly the two objects described above.

## GA4 Realtime production-receipt evidence

Post-publication, GA4 Realtime showed, within one reporting window: `phone_click: 4`, `page_view: 2`, `session_start: 2`, `reservation_click: 1`, `scroll: 1`. This confirms `phone_click` is being received in production end-to-end (website → GTM → GA4). **This count of 4 is an aggregate realtime-window total, not evidence of four firings from a single click** — no duplicate-firing claim is made or supported by this figure. The once-per-click firing behavior is established separately, by the individual GTM Preview evidence above, not by this Realtime aggregate.

## Semantic / scope boundary

Per the pre-existing definition already recorded in observations/O-014.md (unchanged, not re-litigated here): **`phone_click` means user activation of a `tel:` link — it does not mean a telephone call was placed, connected, or completed, and it does not mean a reservation or any conversion occurred.** No broader claim is made in this observation.

## Evidence limitations, recorded explicitly

- The catering fallback `tel:` links remain untested — not evidenced, not claimed.
- Nested-icon-specific DOM targeting inside the footer link was not directly proven — the design relies on Just Links' documented link-resolution behavior (see the GTM configuration rationale above), not on a confirmed icon-click observation.
- The GA4 Realtime count of 4 is an aggregate window count only.
- No new consent-specific claim is made — the `phone_click` tag uses the same `consentSettings: NOT_SET` pattern as the existing, already-validated GA4 event tags (HV-CSD-001), and no additional consent-state testing evidence was collected in this round.

## What this does not establish

This observation validates `phone_click` only. It does **not** establish Production Verified status for `reservation_widget_open`, `directions_click`, `private_dining_enquiry`, or `catering_enquiry`, and it does **not** close the broader first-party measurement-chain gate (measurement/2026-W34-visibility-brief.md, Priority 3), which requires all six named events. With this observation, 2 of those 6 (`reservation_click`, `phone_click`) are Production Verified; the remaining 4 retain their existing status, unchanged.
