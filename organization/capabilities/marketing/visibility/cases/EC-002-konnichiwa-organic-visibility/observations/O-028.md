"n/6" counts the six core events of measurement/2026-W34-visibility-brief.md, Priority 3 — events, not layers. Per that brief, observation O-020 and transformation/HV-IR-001.md (HV-INT-003: "zes vereiste events"), the first-party measurement-chain gate closes when all six are Production Verified.

## G. Outside-Preview GA4 receipt

*Sources: Case Owner statement (test outside the Preview flow); GA4 Realtime observation.*

**Observation:** after publication, a normal production test outside the Preview flow was performed. GA4 Realtime then displayed `catering_enquiry` in the event-name report, with a count of **2** at the time of inspection.

**Interpretation boundary:** the count `2` is not claimed to represent two independent non-Preview production submissions; one event may correspond to the earlier Preview test.

**Inference:** the published Version 10 was the only production path able to emit the event outside Preview, the Preview test fired the tag once (section E), and GA4 showed a count above that single Preview firing after the outside-Preview test. Together with the Case Owner's statement that the test was outside the Preview flow, this establishes GA4 receipt through the published container (condition 3). This inference rests on the Case Owner's sequence of tests; it does not establish exact event counts.
