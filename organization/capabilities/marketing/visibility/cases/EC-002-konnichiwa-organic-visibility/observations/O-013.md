**New finding not previously documented in OC-002 or O-002: the decline is not perfectly monotonic.** All six metrics show the same three-phase shape: a steep decline February→April, a **flat-to-visibly-higher segment around April→May** (clearest in Menucontent bekeken: an estimated rise from ~55 to ~88, close to March's level; also visible in Telefoongesprekken ~65→~68, Interacties ~1,350→~1,380, and Afspraken ~42→~44; Websiteklikken and Opgevraagde routes instead show their flattest segment one month earlier, March→April), then the decline resumes and **steepens June→July**, the single steepest month-over-month drop in the window for every metric.

This directly bears on OC-002's current wording ("declined continuously... did not recover at any point within the window"): that wording is an overstatement for at least Menucontent bekeken, where a real, visually clear partial recovery occurs, and is imprecise for Interacties, Telefoongesprekken, and Afspraken, which show a flat-to-marginally-positive point rather than a strict decline at that one point. The **overall six-month shape** (high in February, lowest in July, large aggregate decline) is unaffected by this finding. See OC-002 Reassessment below.

### E-12 — Measurement definition changes

**Accessible — public documentation, executed this run.** WebSearch and WebFetch of Google's official Business Profile Help page (`support.google.com/business/answer/9918094`), 24 July 2026:

- **Confirmed (official source):** Google's "Views" metric counts unique visitors, not raw impressions. A secondary, non-official source (an SEO blog) claimed this was a *change made in 2026*; the official Google Help page presents unique-visitor counting as the current methodology without indicating a 2026 change date, so **the blog's "changed in 2026" claim is not corroborated by the official source and is not adopted**.
- **Confirmed (official source), one genuine change of unknown date:** Google's own Help page states it changed how "unique direction requests" are counted, to exclude multiple taps, cancelled requests, and spam. **No date is given for when this change took effect.** This means it is *possible* that some portion of the "Opgevraagde routes" decline reflects better spam/duplicate filtering rather than a real drop in requests — but since the change is undated, it cannot currently be placed within, before, or after the Feb–Jul 2026 window, so it **cannot be confirmed or ruled out** as a contributor.
- No evidence was found (official or otherwise) of a change to Interactions, Website clicks, Calls, Menu views, or Appointments metric definitions.

Sources: [Understand your Business Profile performance & insights](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094?hl=en); [Google Business Profile quietly adds offers data to performance metrics docs](https://ppc.land/google-business-profile-quietly-adds-offers-data-to-performance-metrics-docs/) (secondary source, claim not corroborated — see above).
