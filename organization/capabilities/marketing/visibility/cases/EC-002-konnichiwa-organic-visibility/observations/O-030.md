## A. Correction to O-029, section G

O-029 section G stated that the NL homepage `/` and `/omakase-utrecht/` were absent from the page sitemap. Re-inspection shows this was inaccurate, caused by an over-narrow filter in the earlier audit:

- `https://konnichiwa.nl/` **is present** in `page-sitemap.xml`.
- `/omakase-utrecht/` is **not an NL page**: it redirects to `/en/omakase-utrecht/`, which is present in the sitemap.

`/catering-2/` was, and remains, absent. O-029 is not rewritten; this section supersedes only the two inaccurate examples in O-029 section G. O-029's page identities, root cause, routing matrix, navigation findings and theme-code references are unaffected.

## C. Search visibility and history

- **Surfaced by search:** three public web searches (brand + catering, exact `"konnichiwa.nl/catering"`, domain-restricted) surfaced **no** catering URL; other konnichiwa.nl pages did surface. The search tool used is not Google; absence is not evidence of non-indexation.
- **Confirmed indexed:** not established for any of the three URLs; no current Google Search Console access was available.
- **Existing GSC evidence** (evidence/raw/search-console-2026-07-23/Paginas.csv, last three months to 23 July 2026, 55 pages with impressions): **no catering URL**. The only catering-related query in the same export was "teppanyaki catering near me" (1 impression).
- **Inference:** the same export shows EN then served without a language directory (e.g. `/about-us/`, `/store/`) and NL at `/nl/home-nederlands/`, so `/catering/` was then most likely the URL of EN page 75; it recorded no impressions in that window.
- **Backlinks:** not assessed; no backlink data source (Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush) was available. No backlink claim is made.

**Conclusion:** no evidence of search history or value was found for `/catering/`, `/catering-2/` or `/en/catering/`; `/catering-2/` has existed only since 26 September 2026.

## D. Sitemap finding — stale Rank Math sitemap

*Source: `https://konnichiwa.nl/sitemap_index.xml` (Rank Math) and its child sitemaps, 26 September 2026.*

**Observed:**

- Pages are listed in `page-sitemap.xml` (22 URLs: 18 under `/en/`, 4 NL — `/`, `/about-us/`, `/sushi-workshop/`, `/private-dining/`). Listed URLs are final canonical URLs; `/catering/` is correctly not listed.
- Of the NL pages linked from the NL main menu, only `/catering-2/` is absent; it is `index, follow`.
- The sitemap is **stale**: its latest `lastmod` is 2026-09-12. `/private-dining/` is listed with `lastmod` 2026-09-01 although its page metadata shows `dateModified` 2026-09-23; `/en/catering/` is listed with 2026-09-07 although modified 2026-09-26.

**Inference:** `/catering-2/` is absent because the sitemap output has not been refreshed since about 12 September 2026, before page 224 was published. No evidence was found of a Polylang single-language filter, a page-specific exclusion or a noindex setting.

**Unknown (requires WordPress-admin access):** Rank Math sitemap cache settings, any object cache, and Polylang/Rank Math integration settings.

**Classification:** a **site-wide stale-sitemap issue**, not Catering-specific and not part of any Catering remediation. It requires its own bounded diagnosis/refresh gate.

**Separate, undiagnosed note:** `/en/omakase-utrecht/` is served as `en-GB` with a Dutch-language title ("… Japans Restaurant"), and `/omakase-utrecht/`, `/sushi-utrecht/` and `/wagyu-utrecht/` redirect to their `/en/` versions without an NL translation. This may indicate Dutch-content landing pages assigned to EN; it needs a separate check and is not diagnosed here.
