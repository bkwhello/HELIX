## What this does and does not answer

**Answered:** Konnichiwa's site passes Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktop for real visitors over the last 28 days — this is a genuinely positive, verified finding, not assumed. It is not the source of any conversion friction found elsewhere in this case (the three tracking/CTA gaps in observations/O-011.md remain the identified friction points, and they are functional/design gaps, not performance gaps).

**Not yet answered:** Lighthouse lab scores (Performance/Accessibility/Best-Practices/SEO 0–100) were not present in the fetched report content — the lab-test portion of the page appears to load separately/asynchronously from the field-data portion and was not captured. Classification: evidence (field data, real) — no longer a pure blocker. Lab scores remain a minor, distinct gap, not required to resolve the core mobile-performance question this observation was asked to answer.
