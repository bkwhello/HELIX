## Scope boundary

**This evidence validates `reservation_click` only.** It does **not** establish production verification for:
- `phone_click`
- `reservation_widget_open`
- `workshop_click`
- completed Guestplan reservations (i.e., a booking actually being finished)

**Evidence discipline:** `reservation_click` measures reservation-CTA intent / entry into the reservation flow only. It does not prove a completed reservation, and no broader conversion claim is made or upgraded on the basis of this evidence.

## Excluded from scope — Sushi Masterclass CTAs

Repository audit (same session) identified three "Reserveer je plek" / "Reserve your spot" CTAs (`front-page.php`, `template-sushi-masterclass.php`, `template-sushi-masterclass-eng.php`) that do not open Guestplan and are **intentionally excluded** from `reservation_click` by Case Owner decision (26 August 2026) — they represent workshop/masterclass conversion intent, not table-reservation intent, and belong to a separate, not-yet-built measurement taxonomy (`workshop_click` or otherwise). Their future measurement remains separate, out-of-scope future work — not part of this intervention, not otherwise recorded here beyond this boundary note.
