# HV-MP-002 – OC-002 Evidence-Collection Plan (Google Business Profile Decline)
---

## Status

Authorized — Path 1 (decisions/DD-011 amendment, decisions/DD-010). Read-only. Not yet executed.

## Case

EC-002 — Konnichiwa Organic Visibility Growth

## Authorization

Kelvin Wong, case owner, 24 July 2026: "Collect additional evidence for OC-002 before diagnosis." OC-002 remains a Justified Organizational Claim (decisions/DD-010) but is explicitly **not** eligible for diagnosis until this case contains sufficient evidence to explain the decline. This plan does not treat the decline as a diagnosis and does not select interventions. External changes are not authorized; all items below are read-only observation, export, or screenshot review.

## Purpose

Determine whether any evidence exists that connects OC-002 (a six-month, all-metric decline in Google Business Profile engagement, Feb–Jul 2026, observations/O-002.md) to a specific, identifiable cause or to another claim already established in this case (OC-001, OC-003, OC-004, OC-005, OC-006, OC-007). This plan does not presuppose a cause; several items may return "no explanatory evidence found," which is itself a valid, reportable result.

---

## Evidence Items

### 1. Metric-by-metric six-month trend

- **Question:** Do all GBP metrics decline at the same rate and on the same timeline, or do some diverge?
- **Source:** Google Business Profile Performance report, monthly granularity, Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Date range:** Feb 2026 – Jul 2026 (6 months).
- **Method:** Export or screenshot monthly breakdown per metric (calls, website clicks, route requests, bookings, menu views, interactions).
- **Required access:** Kelvin (GBP owner) — read-only export.
- **Limitation:** GBP's own reporting granularity and any retroactive metric redefinitions are outside our control.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** New evidence file (EV-019 or next available ID) with monthly table per metric.
- **Approval requirement:** None beyond this plan's authorization (read-only export).

### 2. Exact decline start date

- **Question:** Is there a single identifiable start date/week for the decline, or is it gradual across the full 6 months?
- **Source:** Same GBP export as item 1, at weekly granularity if available.
- **Date range:** Dec 2025 – Jul 2026 (2 months before the observed window, to catch the actual onset).
- **Method:** Visual/tabular inspection for an inflection point.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** GBP UI may not expose weekly granularity beyond a rolling window.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Annotation on EV-019 noting onset date or "gradual, no single inflection point."
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 3. Branded vs. discovery searches, where available

- **Question:** Is the decline concentrated in "discovery" searches (found via search terms) or "direct/branded" searches (searched for Konnichiwa by name)?
- **Source:** GBP Performance report's search-type breakdown.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Export/screenshot the Direct/Discovery/Branded split if GBP's current reporting UI still offers it.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** Google has been progressively removing this breakdown from the GBP UI; may be unavailable.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Annotation on EV-019, or explicit "not available in current GBP UI" note.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 4. Website clicks

- **Question:** Did website-click volume decline in proportion to overall interactions, or disproportionately?
- **Source:** GBP Performance report.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Already partially covered by observations/O-002.md; confirm per-metric proportionality.
- **Required access:** Kelvin (already supplied for O-002; re-check for consistency with item 1's export).
- **Limitation:** None beyond item 1's.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Comparison note within EV-019.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 5. Calls

- **Question:** Did call-click volume follow the same decline curve?
- **Source:** GBP Performance report.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Included in item 1's monthly export.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** GBP call tracking does not distinguish call outcome (answered, booked, abandoned).
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Row in EV-019's monthly table.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 6. Route requests

- **Question:** Did direction requests decline at the same rate — this metric is less likely to be affected by on-page/profile content changes, so a shared decline curve would point toward a discovery/visibility cause rather than a profile-content cause.
- **Source:** GBP Performance report.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Included in item 1's monthly export.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** None beyond item 1's.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Row in EV-019's monthly table.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 7. Reservation actions (GBP "book" clicks specifically)

- **Question:** Did GBP-originated booking-button clicks decline, and does that trend match or diverge from Guestplan's own reservation volume (observations/O-011.md)?
- **Source:** GBP Performance report booking-action metric; Guestplan reservation-source report.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2026 (GBP) cross-referenced with the 90-day Guestplan window already collected.
- **Method:** Compare GBP booking-click trend to Guestplan's own monthly reservation counts if available.
- **Required access:** Kelvin (both systems).
- **Limitation:** Guestplan's source attribution has known gaps (OC-007) — this comparison can show correlation, not attribution.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Cross-reference note in EV-019; explicit statement that any correlation found is associative, not causal.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 8. Profile / category / menu / hour / link changes

- **Question:** Did anyone (Kelvin, staff, a manager account, or Google itself) change the GBP category, business hours, menu links, website URL, or other profile fields during or before the decline window?
- **Source:** GBP's own edit history / "Business Profile updates" panel, if Google exposes one; otherwise Kelvin's own recollection.
- **Date range:** Nov 2025 – Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Review GBP account activity/change log if available in the GBP dashboard; ask Kelvin directly whether he or staff made any profile edits in this window.
- **Required access:** Kelvin (GBP account access, and his own recollection).
- **Limitation:** Google does not always expose a full edit history to the profile owner; this may rely partly on Kelvin's memory, which should be recorded as such (not treated as a verified log).
- **Expected evidence artifact:** New evidence file or annotation, explicitly marked "GBP change log" vs. "owner recollection" as separate confidence tiers.
- **Approval requirement:** None (read-only review of existing account state/history).

### 9. Review count, score, recency, and response behavior

- **Question:** Did review velocity, average rating, or response rate/time change in a way that could coincide with the decline (Google's algorithm is known to weight review recency and owner responsiveness)?
- **Source:** GBP review section.
- **Date range:** Nov 2025 – Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Export or tabulate review count and average rating at monthly checkpoints if historical values are visible; otherwise record current state and review dates for the most recent 20–30 reviews.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** GBP does not provide a historical rating time series; this may only establish current state, not a trend, unless Kelvin has past screenshots.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** New evidence file with review-recency table.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 10. Photo and post activity

- **Question:** Did the frequency of photo uploads or GBP "Posts" change before or during the decline window (both are known engagement signals Google's algorithm considers)?
- **Source:** GBP Photos and Posts sections.
- **Date range:** Nov 2025 – Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Review upload/post dates visible in the GBP dashboard.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** Historical posting frequency before the most recent items may not be easily visible in the UI.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Annotation in EV-019 or a short standalone note.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 11. Competitor changes

- **Question:** Did a specific nearby competitor (e.g., Kong Izakaya, Japanese Don Dining KOUNOSUKE, both seen in O-003's local-pack observation) newly open, get renovated, run a promotion, or gain a sudden review surge in this window — which could explain a relative (not absolute) decline in Konnichiwa's visibility?
- **Source:** Public GBP listings of named competitors; general web search for news of openings/renovations.
- **Date range:** Nov 2025 – Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Manual review of competitor GBP profiles (review count/date trend) and a general search for relevant local news.
- **Required access:** None beyond public information; WebSearch tool has no Utrecht geo-control (documented limitation, same as O-003's first attempt) so any search-based findings here carry that same caveat.
- **Limitation:** No systematic way to detect all competitor changes; this item can rule things in, not rule things out.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Short note, explicitly framed as non-exhaustive.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 12. Seasonality and prior-period comparison

- **Question:** Is a comparable decline visible in the same Feb–Jul window of a prior year, which would suggest seasonality rather than a case-specific cause?
- **Source:** GBP Performance report, if Google retains more than 6 months of history; Guestplan's own historical reservation data if it goes back further than the current 90-day export.
- **Date range:** Feb–Jul 2025 (prior year), compared to Feb–Jul 2026.
- **Method:** Export prior-year data if available.
- **Required access:** Kelvin.
- **Limitation:** GBP Performance data may not be retained that far back (Google's own UI often caps at ~6–18 months, varies by account); this item may return "unavailable" rather than a comparison.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Annotation on EV-019, or explicit "no prior-year data available" note.
- **Approval requirement:** None.

### 13. Google metric-definition or reporting changes

- **Question:** Did Google change how any GBP metric is counted or defined during this window — a known, recurring source of apparent "declines" that are actually measurement artifacts?
- **Source:** Google's own Business Profile Help/announcements pages; general search for "Google Business Profile metric change 2026" or similar.
- **Date range:** Nov 2025 – Jul 2026.
- **Method:** WebSearch/WebFetch of Google's official GBP help and announcement pages.
- **Required access:** None (public information). WebSearch's US-only geo-control limitation does not materially affect this item, since it concerns a global product-level change, not a local result.
- **Limitation:** Google does not always publicly announce metric-definition changes; absence of a found announcement does not prove none occurred.
- **Expected evidence artifact:** Short note citing any found announcement, or explicit "no relevant announcement found."
- **Approval requirement:** None.

---

## Explicitly Out of Scope for This Plan

- Selecting or implementing any intervention.
- Declaring a cause for the decline, even a tentative one — this plan collects evidence; determining whether that evidence supports a diagnosis is a separate, later, explicitly-authorized step.
- Any change to the live GBP profile, website, or any other external system.
