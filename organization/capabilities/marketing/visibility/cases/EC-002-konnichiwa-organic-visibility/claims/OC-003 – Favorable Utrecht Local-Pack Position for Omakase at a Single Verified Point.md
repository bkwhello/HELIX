#### Organizational Claim

At one verified, genuinely Utrecht-located, logged-out, incognito mobile observation (24 July 2026, 06:41 Europe/Amsterdam), Konnichiwa held position 2 of 3 in Google's local pack for the query "omakase utrecht," behind Kong Izakaya and ahead of Japanese Don Dining KOUNOSUKE.

#### Organizational Relevance

Local-pack placement is a primary discovery surface for "near me"-style dining searches and is the most direct proxy this case has for what a real Utrecht searcher sees. A favorable single observation, if it generalizes, would materially change the priority assigned to the omakase theme in design/HV-VCM-001.md, which currently classifies it as priority 2 based partly on now-superseded, non-geo-controlled evidence.

#### Scope

- Channel: Google local pack (Maps-linked 3-result block) — explicitly not organic web search
- Query: "omakase utrecht" only
- Geography: Utrecht region, as selected/confirmed by Google for this specific search
- Device: iPhone, Google Chrome, incognito, logged out
- Time period: Single point-in-time, 24 July 2026, 06:41 Europe/Amsterdam

#### Evidential Basis

EV-018 is Kelvin's direct, structured report of his own screen: exact query, date, time, Google-confirmed region, device, browser, incognito mode, logged-out state, and the three-result local-pack ranking with Konnichiwa named at position 2. This is treated as Hoog-reliability Direct Organizational Evidence, consistent with how evidence/HV-IV-002.md treats owner-confirmed facts, though it is explicitly noted that no image file was independently viewed by Claude for this specific observation — the record rests on Kelvin's structured report, not independent pixel verification.

#### Confidence

- Level: High for this single observation as reported; Low for any generalization beyond it
- Rationale: The observation method (incognito, logged out, Utrecht-confirmed) is exactly the kind of geo-controlled check this case's earlier proxy attempts (evidence/HV-IV-003.md, the WebSearch check) explicitly lacked and flagged as a limitation. One well-controlled observation is stronger evidence for its specific point/time/device than any number of uncontrolled ones — but it is still one observation.

#### Limitations

- One Utrecht regional measurement point only — not a multi-point grid across the city.
- One device (iPhone/Chrome) — no cross-device comparison.
- One observation time — no time-of-day or day-of-week variation captured.
- Says nothing about organic (non-local-pack) search position — that is O-004/OC-001's domain, a different Google surface entirely.
- Says nothing about review rating as a cause of the position (no causal mechanism tested).
- The two competitors identified (Kong Izakaya, Japanese Don Dining KOUNOSUKE) were not previously present in evidence/HV-IV-006.md's competitor register — this observation surfaces a gap in that register, not yet reconciled.

#### Alternative Interpretations

- Local-pack results can vary meaningfully with small changes in exact device GPS position, even within "Utrecht" as a stated region — this single point may not represent the experience of a searcher on the opposite side of the city.
- Time-of-day (06:41, early morning) is an unusual search time for omakase-dining intent; whether the local pack composition changes at typical dinner-decision times (e.g., early evening) is untested.
- The specific phrasing "omakase utrecht" (lowercase, exact) may not represent how most real searchers phrase this intent (e.g., "beste omakase utrecht," "omakase restaurant utrecht") — untested.

#### Causal Status

Descriptive.

#### Falsification Tests

1. **Is a single observation misrepresented as a stable trend?** This is the primary risk for this claim, and the Organizational Claim wording is deliberately anchored to "at one verified... observation," not "Konnichiwa ranks second" unqualified. Passes on wording; the Explicitly Forbidden Conclusions list in this task names exactly this risk ("Konnichiwa ranks second everywhere in Utrecht," "position is stable") and this claim does not make either statement.
2. **Is the claim broader than its measurement scope?** Checked against Scope — explicitly local-pack only, one query, one point, one device, one time. Not broadened.
3. **Could seasonality explain the condition?** Not applicable to a single point-in-time observation in the way it would apply to a trend; noted as untestable from this data alone.
4. **Could branded demand distort the interpretation?** No — "omakase utrecht" does not contain "Konnichiwa," so this is a non-branded query; not applicable as a distortion risk.
5. **Could device, location, or personalization affect the result?** Yes, directly — this is the central limitation of the claim and is stated three times across Scope, Limitations, and here for emphasis, consistent with the instruction not to let a single geo-controlled point be over-generalized.
6. **Is unavailable data being interpreted as poor performance?** Not applicable — this claim reports a positive, present result, not an absence.
7. **Does conflicting evidence exist?** Yes — the two earlier, non-geo-controlled proxy checks (evidence/HV-IV-003.md, the 24 July WebSearch attempt) suggested a weaker position. This is not treated as contradicting EV-018; it is treated as lower-reliability evidence on a different (or less-controlled) basis, per CR-005's resolution. Both are preserved in observations/O-003.md; neither is deleted.
8. **Would a reasonable challenger accept this confidence level?** Only if the claim's wording strictly preserves the single-observation framing — which it does. A version of this claim that dropped "at one verified... observation" from the Organizational Claim statement would not survive this test.

Outcome: **Survives with Narrowing.** The claim wording itself already reflects the narrowing required (anchored to the single observation); no further wording change was needed beyond what was already present, but the "one observation only" framing is treated as load-bearing and must not be edited out in any downstream use of this claim.

#### Boundaries

OC-003 establishes what one specific, well-controlled observation showed. It does not establish, and must not be used to support:

- that Konnichiwa ranks second everywhere in Utrecht (explicitly forbidden by this task's instructions);
- that this position is stable over time (explicitly forbidden);
- that Konnichiwa has organic position two for anything (explicitly forbidden — this is local-pack, not organic);
- that review rating caused this position (explicitly forbidden, and no mechanism was tested);
- a multi-point Utrecht rank grid (does not exist yet).

evidence/HV-IV-003.md (informal, no geo-control) and the 24 July WebSearch measurement (US-region only, no geo-control), both preserved in observations/O-003.md, both showing a weaker apparent position than EV-018. Resolved for the initial baseline via CR-005 (the geo-controlled observation is weighted higher), not deleted or overwritten.
