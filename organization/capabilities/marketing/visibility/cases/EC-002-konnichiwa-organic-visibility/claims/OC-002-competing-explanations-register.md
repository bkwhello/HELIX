## Summary

Of eleven candidate explanations arising from available evidence, **none reaches "Plausible."** Two (CE-02, CE-03) are Weakly Supported but narrow in scope — neither, even if fully confirmed, explains the full six-metric, six-month pattern. One (CE-09) is Contradicted as a direct mechanism. One (CE-11) is now Unsupported, based on Kelvin's 24 July 2026 Owner Declaration ruling out the thirteen examined operational categories — this narrows, but does not eliminate, "genuine drop in underlying local demand" (CE-10) as a live possibility, since CE-11 addresses supply-side/self-inflicted causes, not demand-side ones. The remaining six are Unassessable, concentrated in exactly the categories this run could not access: profile-change history (CE-05 partially, CE-06), review behavior (CE-07), and any monthly-granularity comparison across sources (CE-01, CE-04, CE-10). This concentration of "Unassessable" in the access-blocked categories is itself the main finding of this register — not that a cause is close to being found, but that the categories most likely to explain a GBP-specific decline remain untested.
