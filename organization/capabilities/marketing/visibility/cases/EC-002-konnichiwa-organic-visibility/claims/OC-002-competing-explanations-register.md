## Summary

Of twelve candidate explanations arising from available evidence, **none reaches "Plausible."** Four (CE-02, CE-03, CE-06, CE-07) are Weakly Supported but each narrow in scope — none, even in combination, explains the full six-metric, six-month pattern. CE-06 and CE-07 are now dated: the confirmed 2026-07-24 screenshot capture date (EV-024, Owner Declaration) let the E-06 review sample be approximately placed within the investigation window and the E-07 post activity be read as likely predating it — a real improvement, but still bounded by sample size (CE-07) and imprecise relative labeling (CE-06). One (CE-09) is Contradicted as a direct mechanism. One (CE-11) is Unsupported, based on Kelvin's 24 July 2026 Owner Declaration ruling out the thirteen examined operational categories. The remaining six (CE-01, CE-04, CE-05, CE-08, CE-10, CE-12) are Unassessable — CE-12 is new: three confirmed, Google-attributed GBP configuration changes exist with a now-dated current snapshot, but the transition date of each change itself remains unknown, so none can be placed relative to the decline window. The recurring limiting factor across this register has narrowed from "dates entirely unknown" to **"dates approximate or partial rather than exact"** — real, increasingly-dated evidence now exists for most categories, which is why several items reach "Weakly Supported" without any reaching "Plausible."
