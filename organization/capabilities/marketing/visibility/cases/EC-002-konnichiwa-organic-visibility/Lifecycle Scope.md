# Lifecycle Scope
---

## HELIX Organizational Engineering Lifecycle (EM-001), extended with a case-specific Evaluation stage

```text
Observation
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Justified Organizational Claims
        ↓
Organizational Understanding
        ↓
Organizational Diagnosis
        ↓
Design
        ↓
Transformation
        ↓
Evaluation
```

"Evaluation" is a case-specific addition beyond EM-001's seven canonical stages, added because EC-002 runs a bounded 30-day execution window that requires an explicit comparison-against-baseline step. It does not replace or bypass any EM-001 stage.

## Lifecycle Gate

No intervention may be described as required until it is supported by a diagnosis. No Organizational Design may proceed without a justified Organizational Diagnosis (EM-001 EP-005). No Transformation may proceed without a justified Design (EM-001 EP-006).

## Fast-Cycle Rule

Each weekly cycle may move through a small Observation → Diagnosis → Design → Transformation loop, provided:

- evidence is attached;
- uncertainty is explicit;
- the change is reversible or separately approved;
- measurement is defined before implementation;
- results are preserved after implementation.

## Termination and Continuation Conditions

EC-002 may terminate when:

- target conditions (measurement/TC-register.md) have been evaluated;
- a repeatable Marketing Visibility operating capability exists;
- ownership has transferred into normal operations;
- evidence and decisions are traceable;
- remaining work no longer requires an Engineering Case.

EC-002 may continue when:

- top-three coverage has not stabilized;
- measurement remains unreliable;
- diagnosis remains unresolved;
- transformation results reveal new organizational conditions.

EC-002 may park when external constraints prevent useful continuation. Parking must preserve state, evidence, unknowns, and the restart condition.
