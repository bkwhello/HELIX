# Explicit Boundaries
---

## In Scope

- Google organic search
- Google local results and Maps
- Google Business Profile
- Konnichiwa website landing pages
- technical indexability and search presentation
- local entity consistency (name, address, phone, hours)
- reviews and review-response routines
- locally relevant citations and earned links
- search-driven content
- reservation, call, and route-request conversion
- weekly measurement and action selection

## Out of Scope

- guaranteed rankings
- paid search as a substitute for organic evidence
- deceptive reviews or incentivized positive reviews
- bulk or purchased backlinks
- unrelated social-media growth
- restaurant operations unrelated to discoverability or conversion
- destructive website changes without approved implementation records
- autonomous publication, messaging, or external account changes without human approval

## Organizational Scope

Restaurant Konnichiwa (Utrecht) only. The sushi shop in Bussum is out of scope; it would require a separate case (see HV-IV-002).

## Temporal Scope

The case investigates the current operational and search-visibility reality of Konnichiwa as observed during the case period beginning 23 July 2026 (this case establishment). Evidence collected under the original 22 July 2026 case establishment (HV-IV-001 through HV-IV-007) remains valid and is treated as prior evidence, explicitly dated.

## Engineering Scope

```text
Observation
→ Evidence
→ Justified Organizational Claims
→ Organizational Understanding
→ Organizational Diagnosis
→ Design
→ Transformation
→ Evaluation
```

No intervention may be described as required until it is supported by a diagnosis. The 30-day execution window does not remove the lifecycle gates — it compresses the work into short evidence and decision cycles (see Lifecycle Scope.md, Fast-Cycle Rule).
