## Authorized Question

DQ-001

## Condition Explained

Within the 21 Apr–21 Jun 2026 Search Console window, Konnichiwa's non-branded organic search position is materially stronger for "teppanyaki utrecht" (4.47) and "omakase utrecht" (4.70) than for "japans restaurant utrecht" (7.38–8.13) and "sushi utrecht" (14.76) — the same contrast established in OC-001, UR-001, and OU-003, re-verified directly against EV-014 in diagnosis/DQ-001-investigation.md, Phase 1.

## Diagnosed Mechanism

The contrast corresponds, ordinally and in both directions, to the number and strength of named competitors Konnichiwa faces in each category, per observations/O-010.md / evidence/HV-IV-006.md's per-category competitor register: omakase has no direct Utrecht competitor at all; teppanyaki has one named competitor with a documented reputation weakness (Restaurant Juliana, Tripadvisor #753/769); the broader "japans restaurant" category includes at least two established, higher-reputation competitors (Ixi Modern Asian Cuisine, 10/10 TheFork; Maneki); and "sushi" has six named competitors with "none dominant." This is recorded as an **associative, evidence-consistent explanation**, not a proven causal mechanism — no controlled test (e.g., competitor removal) exists or could exist within this case's evidence base. The explanation survived a genuine falsification attempt (diagnosis/DQ-001-investigation.md, Phase 5, Attack Competitive Breadth) and explains both ends of the contrast symmetrically, not only the strong or only the weak side.

## Contributing Conditions

- Impression-volume differences across themes (japans restaurant: 1,480 + 284 impressions; sushi: 503; vs. teppanyaki: 375, omakase: 388) are consistent with the broad-category queries drawing genuinely larger, more contested demand pools — compounding, not separate from, the competitive-density finding above.
- Local Entity Relevance (GBP category configuration) is a weak, unresolved contributing factor at most (Weakly Supported, Phase 6) — categories are configured for all four themes, including the two weaker ones, so this does not independently distinguish the contrast, but is not excluded as a minor compounding factor either.

## Rejected Explanations

- **Search-Intent Alignment via dedicated pages (CE-DQ1-A)** — falsified: the two best-performing themes had no dedicated page during the measured window.
- **Query-to-Page Ownership (CE-DQ1-C)** — falsified: `/sushi-utrecht/` is a substantive, non-duplicated, dedicated page and still ranks worst of the four themes.
- **Authority and Prominence (CE-DQ1-E)** — falsified as an explanation of the *contrast*: the only authority signal available (605/625 reviews, CR-006, Open) is a single business-wide figure, structurally incapable of producing a per-theme differential outcome.

## Unresolved Alternatives

- Whether structural "category breadth" (broad, generic terms being inherently harder to rank for, independent of any specific named competitor) contributes separately from the named-competitor crowding found here — the available evidence cannot cleanly separate these two framings, since both predict the same ordinal outcome and no test distinguishes them.
- Full device- or day-level disaggregation of the organic contrast — not available in the current Search Console export; sushi's position rests on only 2 clicks, carrying real residual statistical uncertainty.
- Whether a non-page-based form of search-intent alignment (e.g., homepage copy emphasis, GBP business description wording) contributes independently of page existence — not tested, no evidence available.
- The competitor register's currency and reliability (Medium, single-dated 22 Jul 2026, automated search-based, not independently SERP-verified) — the named competitors' actual current ranking positions for these exact queries were not confirmed.

## Confidence

- Level: **Medium**
- Rationale: the diagnosed mechanism is grounded in positive, dated, category-specific evidence (O-010/HV-IV-006) that survived a genuine falsification attempt and correctly predicts both ends of the contrast — but the underlying competitor register is itself Medium-reliability (automated search, single date, not independently SERP-verified), no query-to-page cross-tabulation exists to fully confirm which page serves which query, no device- or day-level disaggregation exists, and the "category breadth" and "named-competitor crowding" framings remain evidentially entangled. These gaps cap confidence below High without invalidating the finding.

## Limitations

- **E-03** (no monthly search-term composition trend) blocks any claim that discovery-term composition or competitive pressure shifted during the covered window — this diagnosis makes no such claim.
- **E-10** (no prior-year comparison) blocks any seasonal interpretation — not asserted here.
- No Search Console query×page cross-tabulation exists — the specific page serving each query is inferred from the page inventory, not confirmed directly.
- The competitor register (O-010/HV-IV-006) is single-dated, 22 July 2026, and Medium reliability — it does not confirm competitors' actual current SERP positions for these exact queries.
- OC-003's local-pack point is a single observation (one time, one device, one location) and is not generalized beyond omakase.
- CR-006 (605 vs. 625 reviews) remains Open and unreconciled; this diagnosis does not rely on either figure to support its mechanism.
