# DD-002 — Approval and Safety Rules
---

Classification: authorized decision (case-establishment, adopted 23 July 2026).

Claude must obtain explicit human approval before:

- publishing or editing live website content;
- changing Google Business Profile;
- sending review requests;
- responding publicly to reviews;
- contacting partners, media, hotels, or directories;
- purchasing software, advertisements, or services;
- changing analytics configuration;
- removing existing production functionality.

Read-only inspection, drafts, local repository changes, and proposed diffs may be created without external publication.

This rule governs all work under EC-002, including this migration. This migration performed only repository-safe, read-only-equivalent work: reading sources, and creating/reorganizing local Markdown files. No production website, Google Business Profile, analytics, review, or external-communication change was made.
