## Basis for the Verdict

**What is now well-characterized (supports PASSED):**

- The decline's precise monthly shape is now established at finer granularity than O-002's original approximate pass, for all six GBP metrics (observations/O-013.md, E-01/E-02).
- The measurement period is confirmed: February–July 2026, with July confirmed likely partial.
- The affected metrics are fully enumerated: Interacties, Websiteklikken, Opgevraagde routes, Telefoongesprekken, Menucontent, Afspraken — all six, none omitted.
- Obvious reporting artifacts were checked, not merely assumed away: a single-point/partial-month artifact was ruled out as a full explanation (the decline is visible across five full months before the partial July point); Google's official documentation was checked for metric-definition changes (E-12) — one real but undated change was found (direction-request counting), and one blog claim (a 2026 change to Views counting) was checked against the official source and not corroborated.
- Available context was compared: website/landing-page state (E-08), existing competitor evidence (E-09), and cross-source data (E-13) were all reviewed against the window — none currently provides a dated trigger or a comparable overlapping trend.

**What remains a material limitation (supports the Conditions, not FAILED):**

- Four of thirteen evidence items (E-05 profile-change timeline, E-06 review trend, E-07 photo/post activity, E-11 operational context) are **entirely blocked** — no data exists anywhere in the repository, and none was supplied this run. These are exactly the categories most likely to explain *why* a GBP-specific decline would occur, as opposed to merely describing its shape.
- Two further items (E-03 search-discovery composition, E-10 seasonality) are structurally limited to aggregate or unavailable data and cannot be resolved without either a changed GBP interface or a prior-year comparison period that does not currently exist.
- Of ten candidate explanations assessed (claims/OC-002-competing-explanations-register.md), **none reaches "Plausible."** Two reach "Weakly Supported" but are narrow in scope (cannot explain the full six-metric pattern even if fully confirmed); one is "Contradicted" as a direct mechanism; the remaining seven are "Unassessable," concentrated precisely in the blocked categories above.

## Why Not FAILED

FAILED is reserved for cases where the decline cannot be reliably separated from reporting artifacts, where data periods are not comparable, or where no relationship can be responsibly evaluated at all. None of these fully applies: the decline was checked against and is not fully explained by reporting artifacts; the GBP data is internally comparable across its own six months (even though cross-source comparison is limited); and ten candidate explanations were responsibly evaluated and assigned honest statuses (including seven correctly marked Unassessable, rather than skipped or guessed).

## Why Not PASSED (unconditional)

Unconditional PASSED requires support for "at least one bounded relationship suitable for consideration during Organizational Understanding." No candidate explanation in the register currently reaches even "Plausible," let alone a bounded, evidenced relationship. Passing without conditions would overstate what this run established.
