# Purpose

Define the engineering discipline by which HELIX discovers, validates, and preserves architectural knowledge.

The HELIX Engineering Method ensures that architecture is earned through disciplined investigation rather than assumption.

---

# Scope

This method governs the creation of:

- Engineering Investigations
- Architectural Discoveries
- Architecture Decision Records
- Reference Models
- Specifications
- Standards

It applies to every future HELIX capability.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

HELIX engineering is founded on five principles.

1. Observe reality before creating architecture.
2. Discover concepts rather than invent them.
3. Validate every architectural claim.
4. Preserve engineering evidence.
5. Protect architectural integrity.

---

The HELIX Engineering Method progresses through a sequence of engineering responsibilities.

Each stage produces the engineering artifact required by the next stage.

No stage shall be skipped.

## Validation of the Engineering Method

The HELIX Organizational Engineering Process shall remain open to empirical challenge through application to organizational reality.

Validation is not a binary achievement.

HELIX accumulates justified validation confidence through inspectable, traceable, challengeable, and revisable evidence produced under explicitly observed conditions.

Engineering Cases produce validation evidence.

They do not independently establish universal validity.

Failures, contradictions, and unexplained divergences shall remain visible as engineering evidence and may require refinement of the Engineering Method.

## Constitutional Responsibilities of the Organizational Engineer

The primary responsibility of the Organizational Engineer is to establish justified, evidence-based claims about organizational reality.

Understanding, diagnosis, design, and organizational transformation shall be based upon these claims.

No engineering recommendation shall precede justified organizational claims.

## Engineering Artifacts

The primary engineering artifact of the HELIX Engineering Discipline is the Organizational Claim.

Organizational Claims shall:

- be supported by evidence,
- be explicitly justified,
- remain open to validation,
- remain open to refutation,
- remain open to refinement.

Engineering Understanding shall emerge from collections of validated Organizational Claims.

### Engineering Principle

Engineering knowledge shall be expressed through justified Organizational Claims rather than undocumented understanding or opinion.

## Organizational Understanding

Organizational Understanding emerges from the coherent organization of justified Organizational Claims.

Understanding shall not be treated as a subjective mental state but as an inspectable engineering construct.

Engineering diagnosis shall be based upon Organizational Understanding rather than isolated Organizational Claims.

## Organizational Diagnosis

Organizational Diagnosis is the justified engineering assessment of organizational condition derived from Organizational Understanding.

Diagnosis shall:

- be based upon validated Organizational Understanding,
- remain evidence-based,
- remain open to refinement,
- precede Organizational Design.

Diagnosis shall not prescribe solutions.

Its responsibility is to establish organizational condition.

## Organizational Design

Organizational Design is the justified engineering specification of an intended future organizational state constrained by Organizational Diagnosis.

Design shall:

- be derived from justified diagnosis,
- specify the intended future organizational architecture,
- remain internally coherent,
- remain traceable to diagnosis,
- precede Organizational Transformation.

Organizational Design shall not implement change.

Its responsibility is to specify the future organizational state.
