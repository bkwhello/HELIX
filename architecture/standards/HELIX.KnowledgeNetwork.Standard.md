**Standard ID:** HELIX-KNOW-004

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Architecture Domain

**Classification:** Capability Structure Standard

---

# Purpose

This standard defines the HELIX Knowledge Network (HKN), the organizational structure through which Knowledge Assets are connected by governed Knowledge Relationships.

The Knowledge Network enables organizations to navigate, understand, trace, and continuously improve their organizational knowledge.

---

# Scope

This standard defines:

- The HELIX Knowledge Network
- Network responsibilities
- Network principles
- Network integrity
- Network navigation
- Network traceability

This standard does not define:

- Knowledge Assets
- Knowledge Relationships
- Knowledge Governance
- Knowledge Lifecycle
- Knowledge Versioning
- Knowledge Addressing

These are defined in separate standards.

---

# Definition

The HELIX Knowledge Network (HKN) is the emergent organizational structure formed by governed Knowledge Assets and governed Knowledge Relationships.

The Knowledge Network is not an organizational object.

It is the organizational knowledge landscape that emerges from the relationships between Knowledge Assets.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Organizational intelligence emerges from connected organizational knowledge.

Knowledge Assets provide organizational knowledge.

Knowledge Relationships provide organizational context.

The Knowledge Network enables organizational understanding.

---

# Responsibilities

The Knowledge Network shall:

- organize organizational knowledge
- provide navigability
- provide traceability
- preserve organizational context
- support organizational reasoning
- support knowledge discovery
- remain implementation-independent

---

# Network Principles

## Connected Knowledge

Knowledge Assets shall not exist in isolation.

Every Knowledge Asset should participate in one or more Knowledge Relationships.

---

## Organizational Context

Meaning emerges through relationships.

The network provides context beyond individual Knowledge Assets.

---

## Explainability

Every organizational decision should be traceable through the Knowledge Network.

Example:

Vision

↓

Mission

↓

Objectives

↓

Initiatives

↓

Projects

↓

Execution

---

## Discoverability

Consumers should be able to discover related Knowledge Assets through governed relationships.

---

## Traceability

Every Knowledge Asset shall support bidirectional traceability.

Consumers must be able to answer:

- What supports this?
- What depends on this?
- What changed because of this?

---

## Integrity

The Knowledge Network shall maintain structural integrity.

Broken or unmanaged relationships are not permitted.

---

# Network Characteristics

The HELIX Knowledge Network is:

- Connected
- Governed
- Traceable
- Explainable
- Navigable
- Reusable
- Scalable
- Implementation Independent

---

# Network Rules

The Knowledge Network shall not duplicate organizational knowledge.

Knowledge exists only within Knowledge Assets.

The Network exists only through governed Knowledge Relationships.

---

# Organizational Navigation

The Knowledge Network shall support navigation between related organizational knowledge.

Examples include:

Vision

→ Mission

→ Strategic Objectives

→ Policies

→ Procedures

→ Work Instructions

Navigation shall follow organizational meaning rather than storage location.

---

# Organizational Reasoning

The Knowledge Network enables organizations to answer questions such as:

Why does this policy exist?

Which Knowledge Assets support this process?

Which capabilities depend on this decision?

Which Knowledge Assets are affected by this change?

Organizational reasoning shall be based upon governed Knowledge Relationships.

---

# Architectural Independence

The Knowledge Network is independent of:

- Graph databases
- SQL databases
- Vector databases
- File systems
- AI models
- Programming languages

Technology may implement the Knowledge Network but shall never define it.

---

# Compliance

A HELIX Knowledge Network is compliant only when:

- all Knowledge Assets comply with the Knowledge Asset Standard
- all Knowledge Relationships comply with the Knowledge Relationship Standard
- network integrity is maintained
- organizational traceability is preserved

# Guiding Statement

The HELIX Knowledge Network is the organizational structure through which Knowledge Assets become organizational intelligence.

It preserves context, enables reasoning, supports traceability, and provides the foundation for organizations to learn, adapt, and continuously improve independently of the technologies used to implement it.

# Network Integrity

The HELIX Knowledge Network shall preserve organizational integrity.

The following rules apply:

- Every Knowledge Asset shall be reachable through one or more Knowledge Relationships.
- Orphaned Knowledge Assets shall be reported.
- Broken Knowledge Relationships shall be detected.
- Circular relationships shall be explicitly defined or prohibited.
- Network integrity shall be continuously validated.
